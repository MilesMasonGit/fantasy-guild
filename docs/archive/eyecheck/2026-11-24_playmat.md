# Eye-check: The playmat declutter, combat, loot trail and level-up flash

Build: `_builds/2026-11-24-playmat/Fantasy Guild.exe`. Looked at by the owner on 25 Nov 2026.

## 1. Drag only; right-click opens

- How: Left-click a Token and a hero on the mat. Right-click a Token, then a hero. Click a hero on the hero bar. Drag a hero onto a bush, then drag them onto bare ground.
- See: Left-click does nothing and nothing gets a gold outline. Right-click opens the Token's card or the hero's drawer; a hero bar click opens their drawer. Dragging is the only way to give orders. Dropped on bare ground the flag lands where you let go, but the hero stands a little beside it: say in your note whether they should stand right on the spot.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 2. The Token card

- How: Right-click a Token with a long card, then a Blacksmith from the QA panel with no recipe chosen, pick Copper Ingot.
- See: A stepped tail on the card points at its Token, which comes to the front with a gold outline. Scroll arrows show only while there is more that way. The Blacksmith's card opens on its recipe list; once chosen it opens on its face with Change recipe.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 3. Reach rings after a rest

- How: Rest the pointer on a Copper Mine, then sweep quickly across several Tokens. Drag a camp across the mat.
- See: The name shows at once, the ring and dots only after the pointer rests about half a second; a quick sweep shows no rings. Dots only on what the Token affects (the mine's copper veins, not the coal or stone). A dragged Token's ring shows at once.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 4. Job areas

- How: Hire a few heroes from the QA panel and order them onto neighbouring bushes and trees, one onto a Blacksmith with no Coal. Then make the window smaller.
- See: Each job gets a parchment-toned area round the Token and its hero, fading softly at the edge, no ring line; overlapping areas merge into one. A resting Token half inside is toned on that half only. The stuck job looks the same (its '!' tells it). In a smaller window the areas look a little roomier: say if too loose.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 5. Names, bar and marks

- How: Look at the mat with heroes working, rest on a Blacksmith with a recipe chosen, and place enough spawners to fill the Token cap.
- See: Names sit on a see-through dark strip with a subtle rainbow sweeping slowly across the letters; reach dots have the dark backing too. A worked Token shows a green bar with its cycle time (3.0s), grey when stuck. No plates, timers or charges on the mat, no hover tooltips. The '!' sits still in the Token's centre; a full cap gives a yellow '!' that goes once you hover that spawner. Resting on a station shows the gear in its centre and a bubble with what it makes; the card has a line saying what is wrong.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 6. Banners in a corner

- How: Use the QA panel to hire two heroes and knock one out. Click the icon at the top right, then click it again, then hire another hero.
- See: A show/hide icon at the top right with a count, red when a problem waits. Shown: every banner listed down from it; a click on one clears it. Hidden: a new banner peeks out for 5 seconds, then tucks back into the count. Your choice is remembered.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 7. Top bar and Controls

- How: Look at the top bar. Click the Controls button at the bottom left; try Demolish, hover each Find row, type copper in the search box; then click outside.
- See: No frame on the top bar, Tabletop Guild on the left, the place name on the right, no Token count or Upkeep. The hero bar stays centred. The Controls panel opens upward; Find rows light every match while hovered, the search lights matches and fades the rest; Escape or a click outside closes it; with Demolish on, the button shows a red border.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 8. The enemy's card

- How: Place a Crab (or any enemy) from the QA panel and right-click it. Then send a hero to fight it and look again.
- See: No hover card over enemies. The card shows level, style, health, hit, moves, resistances, how often it attacks and its drops; its kinds read '? fight one to learn' until a hero has fought one, then show.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 9. The combat look

- How: Fight a goblin with a hero, open the fight window for two heroes.
- See: Every health bar is green above half, amber to a quarter, red below (hero bar, drawer, fight window, fighters on the mat). Hits look and time the same in the window and on the mat (the number when the blow lands). Every hero with an open fight window has a lit fight button. Say if the colour lines or the timing should change.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 10. The loot trail

- How: In the QA panel pick an item, enter 1 and press Drop, then 12000, then 3000000; click each pile to collect it.
- See: The flying item trails 1, then 5, then 7 shadows in its own colour, darker toward the tail, with a light sprinkle of sparkles. As it lands, light sparkles burst out from the notice to the left and rain down, longer and denser for bigger piles, and the amount (+1, +12k, +3m) pops out of the notice's left side, flashes and fades above the sparkles.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 11. Loot lands at the edge

- How: Order a hero onto a bush and watch the drops.
- See: Drops land on the edge of the job's parchment area, not right beside the bush, and pile up there.
- Verdict: **ok**

## 12. The level-up flash

- How: Hire a hero, press Level all +1 until a skill reaches 5, then Level all +10.
- See: At level 5 a small flash: three glowing rings spread from the hero and fade, with sparkles. Passing 10 a big one: five rings reaching further and many sparkles. It starts with the level-up float.
- Verdict: **change** — I want the same dramatic effect for every time the hero levels. Also, make it last longer.

## 13. Limes before the first fight

- How: New game with lessons on; follow Limes's lessons to the first fight (or on an older save, reach lesson 8).
- See: Before Now a little fight!, Limes says: Careful! If your hero is Defeated, you lose a quarter of each food and drink they brought. Then: And each piece of gear they wear has a 1 in 10 chance to break. Win, and you lose nothing!

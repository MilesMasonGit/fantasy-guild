## 1. Plain-language summary for the owner

**The fighting itself is cheap.** Eight heroes all in fights at once, on the
300-Token torture board, cost the engine about **0.03 ms a tick** — around 2 %
of the realistic board's budget. Each sword swing takes 3 µs. Statuses, timed
effects and the wounded timer together cost under 0.01 ms a tick. None of these
is where the game's time goes.

**The expensive moment is a kill, and it has nothing to do with the fight.**
When a monster with its last charge dies, the game tells the board "this hero
moved" (they didn't) *and* "the neighbourhood changed", and each of those
rebuilds the buffs around that spot. On the realistic board a kill costs
**about 3–4 ms on its own**, so the tick a kill lands in misses the "no tick
over 4 ms" target. On the big board with one board-wide buff, **every kill
freezes the engine for 120–220 ms** — a visible hitch each time a goblin dies.
About half of that is the fake "hero moved" message, which is a one-line
problem in this territory (CR3-250); the other half is R3's rebuild cost
(CR3-004).

**Loot on the floor is safe in practice, but not in principle.** Dropped items
merge into piles, so even on the torture board with a Bank that refuses
everything, the floor never held more than ~30 piles. But if the Bank is full
*and* many different item types drop in many places, the floor's tidy-up gets
quadratically slower: 0.03 ms at 40 piles, 1.6 ms at 400, 6.7 ms at 800. You
have 64 item types and 64 Bank slots at rank 0, so a full Bank is reachable
(CR3-254).

**Statuses and "live effects" are still two separate systems** — you deferred
merging them (V7b.3, 2026-09-09) and that stands. The cost is fine; the problem
is that each "cure" only reaches one of the two: being defeated clears
statuses but not timed effects, and the `Removes` rule clears timed effects but
not statuses (CR3-251). Timed effects also forget their combat numbers after a
save/load, and their clocks use real-world time, so they run out while the game
is closed (CR3-252). No shipped content applies either kind yet, so no player
can hit these today.

**Things that look like they work but don't quite:**
- A kill's loot ignores "more yield", "double loot" and "bonus drop" rules,
  even though the support Tokens beside the monster still wear down per kill
  (CR3-256, needs your ruling).
- Every game load wipes any rules authored on the Guild Hall, because the
  Wishing Well upgrade rewrites the Hall's definition (CR3-257, needs your
  ruling).
- `GLOBAL_COMBAT_XP_MULTIPLIER` in the tuning file does nothing (CR3-261).
- Working out a hero's level from XP gets slow at high levels — 0.2 ms per XP
  award at level 99 — because a lookup table built for it is never used, and
  round 2's ticket for it was closed by mistake (CR3-258).

**A future cost to know about:** once items carry effects, every working hero
re-reads their whole loadout ten times a second. Measured with six effect items
each: ~0.14 ms and ~114 KB of garbage per tick for 8 heroes (CR3-253).

### 3.5 Loot flood — is `SpriteLayer` bounded?

| Run (S3 board, 6,000 ticks = 10 game-min, checkpoint every 500) | Sprites over time | `sprite_layer` ms/tick |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SpriteLayer.tick` µs/tick | 29 | 147 | 484 | 1,626 | **6,677** |

Quadratic: every tick over the cap sorts the whole list and each refused
`collectSprite` does a linear `find` (`:414`). Each sprite is one page element in
`SpriteLayerView` (R6). CR3-254.

### 3.6 Statuses vs live effects — two systems

Both run: `status_effects` and `live_effects` are separate tick handlers
(`EngineBootstrap.js:208-212, 240-242`), each with its own 5 s clock
(`StatusEffectSystem.js:29,103-106`; `LiveEffects.js:67,333-336`). Owner deferred
the merge (grammar v2 roadmap V7b.3, 2026-09-09: *"I want the system to be more
complete first"*), so **not a ticket in itself**. Cost: ~0.004 ms/tick combined.
**Shipped content applies neither**: 0 `statusOnHit` on any item or enemy
(`enemyProfile.js:103-124` builds none), 0 `applies` statements, 0 library
effects with a duration in `data/`. Double-application risk is low (an
`Applies` carries either `statusId` or `effectId`, never both —
`StatusApplication.js:154-165`); the real risks are the asymmetric cleanses
(CR3-251) and the reload gap (CR3-252).

## 4. Tickets

### CR3-250 — A kill that empties an enemy rebuilds two neighbourhoods, one of them for a hero who did not move
- **Category / Severity / Effort**: HPB · **P1** · S
- **Impact / Confidence / Score**: 5 · 1.0 (measured) · 5.0
- **Risk**: medium — needs test: the hero must still re-choose and the UI must still redraw after a kill (a regression test on `resolveVictory`'s events; see CR3-266)
- **Where**: `src/systems/board/BoardCombat.js:543` (`HERO_MOVED` with the *enemy's* spot, "the hero's flag chooses again on its next pass") and `:544` (`ADJACENCY_DIRTY`); handlers `TileModifiers.js:125-131`, `BoardRunner.js:782-784`
- **What**: the only engine subscriber of `HERO_MOVED` is `TileModifiers`, which rebuilds around the hero's last and current points. `Flags` does not subscribe — it re-chooses on its own tick — so the engine-side effect of line 543 is purely a second rebuild. With a board-reach aura each rebuild is a whole-mat quadratic rebuild.
- **Evidence**: spike `r4-micro` §4, event-timed: S2 kill tick median 2.8–4.2 ms (max 8.2) — with the rest of the S2 tick (p50 1.33 ms) the tick carrying a kill lands at ~4–5.5 ms, **missing §4.4's S2 max ≤ 4 ms**; `HERO_MOVED` 1.6 ms of it. S3 + aura: **123–222 ms per kill**, `HERO_MOVED` 103 ms + `ADJACENCY_DIRTY` 99 ms. All-heroes-fighting S3: `tickToken` 1.42 ms/tick at 0.012 kills/tick.
- **Fix sketch**: stop publishing `HERO_MOVED` for a hero who has not moved (or make `TileModifiers`' handler skip when the hero's point is unchanged — R3's file); keep `TILE_CHANGED`, which the UI already hears. The same pattern exists at `Charges.js:205` for ordinary Token depletion (R3). The remaining `ADJACENCY_DIRTY` cost is CR3-004.
- **Hypothesis to test**: S2 kill tick halves; S3+aura kill drops to ~100 ms; after CR3-004 both < 1 ms.
- **Owner session**: R4 (the publish), R3 (the rebuild)
- **Status**: 🔲 Open

- **Category / Severity / Effort**: WIRE · P2 · S

## 7. Owner questions (multiple choice, recommendation first)

**Q1 — Should a kill's loot follow the same "more yield / double loot / bonus drop" rules a station's output does?** (CR3-256)
- **(A) Recommended: yes.** D-129 already says a kill is a cycle, and support Tokens already wear per kill; kill loot would go through the same output rules. Small change; makes existing rules mean the same thing everywhere.
- (B) No — combat loot stays fixed. Then the editor should warn when such a rule can only reach enemies, and support Tokens arguably shouldn't wear per kill.
- (C) Decide when real combat content is authored.

**Q2 — A timed effect ("+2 Armour for 10 minutes"): whose minutes?** (CR3-252)
- **(A) Recommended: game time** — it pauses when the game does and stops while the game is closed, like wounds and quest timers already do.
- (B) Real time — it keeps running while the game is closed (today's behaviour, by accident).
- (C) Park it with the Time Bank work.

**Q3 — When a hero is defeated, should timed effects be cleared as statuses are?** (CR3-251)
- **(A) Recommended: yes, all of them**, good and bad — matches "a forced retreat cleanses every status" and the effects-robustness plan; and `Removes` should reach statuses too until the two systems merge.
- (B) Clear only harmful ones — keeps bought buffs, needs a "harmful" flag on library effects.
- (C) Leave as is until the V7b merge.

**Q4 — The Guild Hall's Wishing Well rewrites the Hall's definition on every load.** (CR3-257)
- **(A) Recommended: keep the Wishing Well's water as an engine-side extra that does not touch the Hall's authored rules**, so anything authored on the Hall in the editor survives.
- (B) Keep it; mark the Hall "not authorable" in the editor.
- (C) Make the Wishing Well an ordinary authored rule on the Hall that the upgrade rank scales.

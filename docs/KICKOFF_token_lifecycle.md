We're doing an open-minded, design-only brainstorming session about how to best
rework the **Token lifecycle** in Fantasy Guild: how Tokens are spawned, how they
exist on the free playmat, and what happens when they are exhausted. **No code this
session.**

Start by reading `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/HANDOFF_token_lifecycle.md`. That handoff
covers my goals, the current system and the challenges.

Two things matter most:

1. **The existing Map purchase-and-burst system, the Vault and Tray, and the
   Exploration/Construction idea are all meant to be challenged and changed.**
   Don't treat them as foundations. Earlier ideas in the handoff are starting
   material, not decisions.
2. **Brainstorm widely before narrowing.** Show me several genuinely different
   models, not variations of one, and ask me which appeal.

Please interview me with the multiple-choice question tool, with your
recommendation first and the trade-offs spelled out. Don't commit anything I
haven't chosen: if I'm unsure, record it as leaning or open. When we've found a
direction, capture it in a concept doc.

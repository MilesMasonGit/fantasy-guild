# Content roadmap: Smithing

*Part of session 3 of [concept_content_roadmap.md](concept_content_roadmap.md). Names and rough levels are decided here; quantities, times, damage and prices are the ledger's. Checked by a script: each pick before the metal it mines, each main metal's base set at the same offset, the cut items gone.*

## The owner's rulings (2026-11-04)

- **"I generally want each new tier to start every 20 levels. Copper is 1-19, Iron is 20-39, etc. We'll fit silver and gold in where it fits."**
- **"We have very limited space in our recipe list, so I don't want you to clutter it with items that have no practical purpose."**
- **Level 1 ingot, level 2 dagger, level 3 woodaxe** (brief 480); "iron dagger at level 22".
- **The base set** is settled (below). **The Chisel leaves the game**: gems need no tool to be cut.
- **"Some base items will be available in every kind, such as chestplate and pickaxe. Others will be metal specific (a Cleaver may only be Iron)."**
- **Nails: copper, iron and mythril only. Wire, chains and gears** may serve later Science recipes.
- **Not items**: Sickle, Greatsword, Fittings, Hinges, Hoops, Hooks, Cooking Pot, Throwing Axe, Mirror, Spear and the like.
- **Silver and gold don't get weapon sets**: "they will smith some components, perhaps some silver weapons."
- **Gloves and Boots are called Plate Gloves and Plate Boots**, and get hero slots (T-303).
- **Bows and arrows move to Fletching; Smithing makes arrowheads. Wands move to Enchanting.**
- **Bands from Smithing, gems set by Crafting. Gaps stay open for items later skills need.**

## The shape

- **Metal levels**: copper 1, iron 20, silver 30, mythril 40, gold 50, adamantine 60, darkmetal 80.
- **Base set, on copper, iron, mythril, adamantine and darkmetal, at the same place in each tier**: Ingot at the tier's start, then Dagger +2, Woodaxe +3, Pickaxe +4, Sword +6, Helmet +7, Arrowheads +8, Plate Gloves +9, Plate Boots +11, Shield +12, Chestplate +13 (counted from a round 20, 40, 60, 80; copper from 0). So an improved version arrives exactly 20 levels after the last.
- **Beyond it**: Nails on copper, iron and mythril. Kunai on copper and iron. Cleaver and Mace on iron.
- **81 recipes and 18 open levels**, left for what later skills need: 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 75, 76, 77, 78, 79, 81, 85, 90, 94, 95, 96, 97.
- **Handles use that metal's wood**, so Forestry and Smithing climb together.
- **Rules kept**: ingots at the Furnace, everything else at the Smithy; better stations are faster, not new lists; coal is the fuel.

## Art

**32 have, 38 adapt, 11 new.** You have every ingot, pick and woodaxe, five plates, two swords, the iron cleaver (`battleaxe_iron`), and steel gloves, boots and helmet, used here for iron. New shapes: Dagger, Arrowheads, Kunai, Shield, Mace, Wire, Gears, Ring and Amulet Bands, and the two final pieces. Most other entries recolour those, using `color_mythril`, `color_addy` and `color_darkmetal`. `glove_leather`, `boot_leather` and `boot_slipper` wait for other skills; the `chisel_*` and `tongs` pictures go unused.

## Moved or changed from today

- **Retire**: Copper and Iron Sickles (⚠️ anything needing a sickle must stop), Greatswords, Copper Cleaver, and Bows and Wands from the Smithy. ⚠️ Combat lists the Greatsword as Crush; the Mace takes over, and Melee has no Stab now the Spear is cut. Today's content packs change.
- **Ingots move**: Iron 15 becomes 20, Silver 30 stays, Gold 45 becomes 50, Mythril 60 becomes 40, Adamantine 75 becomes 60, Darkmetal 90 becomes 80.
- **Mythril, Adamantine and Darkmetal gain their sets**; only their picks exist today.
- ⚠️ **Kunai used as ammo is new for Stealth**: only arrows work that way today, and combat v2 set aside Throwing Daggers. Amends [concept_combat_v2.md](concept_combat_v2.md) if kept.

## Open links

- **Fletching**: bows and arrows, using Arrowheads. **Enchanting**: wands.
- **Crafting**: gems cut with no tool and set into 24 pieces of jewellery; uses for Nails. **Science**: Wire, Chain and Gears.
- **Gloves and boots** (T-303): slots, numbers and the hero sheet. Leather and cloth versions come with Crafting.
- **Farming**: no sickle, so check what gates its harvests.
- **Woodaxes**: a copper axe takes every tree today, so better axes need a reason (wood and buildings session).
- **Open levels**: left for items later skills need; any still empty bring no new recipe. Copper has six in a row (14 to 19) before iron.
- **The ledger**: an XP curve to fit, as for Cooking.

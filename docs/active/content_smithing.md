# Content roadmap: Smithing

*Part of session 3 of [concept_content_roadmap.md](concept_content_roadmap.md). Names and rough levels are decided here; quantities, times, damage and prices are the ledger's. Checked by a script: each pick before the metal it mines, each main metal's base set at the same offset, the cut items gone.*

## The owner's rulings (2026-11-04)

- **"I generally want each new tier to start every 20 levels. Copper is 1-19, Iron is 20-39, etc. We'll fit silver and gold in where it fits."**
- **"We have very limited space in our recipe list, so I don't want you to clutter it with items that have no practical purpose."**
- **Level 1 ingot, level 2 dagger, level 3 woodaxe** (brief 480); "iron dagger at level 22".
- **The base set** is settled (below). **The Chisel leaves the game**: gems need no tool to be cut.
- **"Some base items will be available in every kind, such as chestplate and pickaxe. Others will be metal specific (a Cleaver may only be Iron)."**
- **Nails: copper, iron and mythril only.** Gears serve Construction. **Silver and Gold Chain make the amulets, in place of Amulet Bands; Wire and Iron Chain leave the list until Science needs them** (owner, 2026-11-05).
- **Better Woodaxes fell better trees**, as picks do; **Darkmetal is needed to cut Ebony** (owner, 2026-11-05).
- **Not items**: Sickle, Greatsword, Fittings, Hinges, Hoops, Hooks, Cooking Pot, Throwing Axe, Mirror, Spear and the like.
- **Silver and gold don't get weapon sets**: "they will smith some components, perhaps some silver weapons."
- **Gloves and Boots are called Plate Gloves and Plate Boots**, and get hero slots (T-303).
- **Bows and arrows move to Fletching; Smithing makes arrowheads. Wands move to Enchanting.**
- **Silver Arrowheads yes, gold no** (Fletching session).
- **Bands from Smithing, gems set by Crafting. Gaps stay open for items later skills need.**

## Amended 2026-11-21 (Systems Audit, T-586, T-588, T-566, T-568)

The owner's rulings from the audits of Stealth ([A18](audit/A18_stealth.md)) and Melee ([A15](audit/A15_melee.md)).

- **Kunai are a Stealth weapon kind that spends itself**: each throw uses one, carried and taken from the Bank like arrows. When only one is left the hero keeps it and fights at half damage, as Magic does with no Motes (the half on the tuning page, T-253). Copper and Iron Kunai stay on the list where they are. **Daggers need nothing.**
- **The Iron Dagger is Smithing 22**, as ruled on 2026-11-04 (today's recipe says 16 and moves with the pack). It is the copper dagger's improved version, one tier later.
- **Every weapon asks a level in its own style by its metal** (T-566), by the weapon tier table in [CONTENT_PHILOSOPHY](../reference/CONTENT_PHILOSOPHY.md): a dagger asks Stealth, a sword Melee. That is the level to wield it, apart from the Smithing level to make it. The wield levels follow the new metal ladder below (copper 1, iron 20, silver 30 …), not T-566's "iron 15" (planning agent's call 2026-11-21).
- **Two-handed weapons out-damage one hand of the same metal** (T-568), but this plan cuts the Greatsword, Melee's only two-handed weapon: **the rule waits for the first two-handed weapon** a plan brings; no new item is added for it now (owner 2026-11-21).

## The shape

- **Metal levels**: copper 1, iron 20, silver 30, mythril 40, gold 50, adamantine 60, darkmetal 80.
- **Base set, on copper, iron, mythril, adamantine and darkmetal, at the same place in each tier**: Ingot at the tier's start, then Dagger +2, Woodaxe +3, Pickaxe +4, Sword +6, Helmet +7, Arrowheads +8, Plate Gloves +9, Plate Boots +11, Shield +12, Chestplate +13 (counted from a round 20, 40, 60, 80; copper from 0). So an improved version arrives exactly 20 levels after the last. **Two exceptions**: the Adamantine and Darkmetal Woodaxes come straight after their ingots, since Mahogany and Ebony need them.
- **Beyond it**: Nails on copper, iron and mythril. Kunai on copper and iron. Cleaver and Mace on iron. Harpoons on adamantine and darkmetal (Fishing).
- **76 recipes and 23 open levels**, left for what later skills need: 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 54, 55, 57, 58, 70, 74, 75, 76, 77, 78, 79, 85, 90, 94, 95, 96, 97.
- **Handles use that metal's wood**, so Forestry and Smithing climb together. The Adamantine and Darkmetal Woodaxes use the wood before, or each would need itself to be made.
- **Which axe fells what**: Copper fells Oak and Fir, Iron fells Birch and Maple, Mythril fells Cedar, Adamantine fells Mahogany, Darkmetal fells Ebony.
- **Everything is made at the Blacksmith** (owner, 2026-11-04; [Construction](content_construction.md)); its variants have different benefits, not new lists; coal is the fuel.

## Art

**31 have, 35 adapt, 10 new.** You have every ingot, pick and woodaxe, five plates, two swords, the iron cleaver (`battleaxe_iron`), and steel gloves, boots and helmet, used here for iron. New shapes: Dagger, Arrowheads, Kunai, Shield, Mace, Gears, Ring Bands, and the two final pieces. Most other entries recolour those, using `color_mythril`, `color_addy` and `color_darkmetal`. `glove_leather`, `boot_leather` and `boot_slipper` wait for other skills; the `chisel_*` and `tongs` pictures go unused.

## Moved or changed from today

- **Retire**: Copper and Iron Sickles (⚠️ anything needing a sickle must stop), Greatswords, Copper Cleaver, and Bows and Wands from the Smithy. ⚠️ Combat lists the Greatsword as Crush; the Mace takes over, and Melee has no Stab now the Spear is cut. Today's content packs change.
- **Ingots move**: Iron 15 becomes 20, Silver 30 stays, Gold 45 becomes 50, Mythril 60 becomes 40, Adamantine 75 becomes 60, Darkmetal 90 becomes 80.
- **Mythril, Adamantine and Darkmetal gain their sets**; only their picks exist today.
- **Kunai are kept** (owner, 2026-11-21, T-586): a thrown Stealth weapon kind, one spent a throw, built with arrows and Motes as one ammo rule (director). Amends [concept_combat_v2.md](concept_combat_v2.md), which set aside Throwing Daggers.

## Open links

- **Fletching**: bows and arrows, using Arrowheads. **Enchanting**: wands.
- **Crafting**: gems cut with no tool and set into 24 pieces of jewellery; uses for Nails. **Science**: may ask for the possible list above.
- **Gloves and boots** (T-303): slots, numbers and the hero sheet. Leather and cloth versions come with Crafting.
- **Farming**: no sickle, so check what gates its harvests.
- **Woodaxes**: a copper axe takes every tree today. The game can already ask for a better one, so the trees just need setting when the Forestry pack is made.
- **Open levels**: left for items later skills need; any still empty bring no new recipe. Copper has six in a row (14 to 19) before iron.
- **The ledger**: an XP curve to fit, as for Cooking.

## Building it (2026-11-21, content agent)

- **In the first Smithing pack**: the ladder with handles, the weapons and armour of the base set on every main metal, the Iron Mace, the Silver Dagger and Sword, the Masterwork Blade; the Greatswords, Copper Cleaver and Sickles retire. Damage and resistances follow the copper and iron steps until the ledger settles them; the new metals' blades use the iron sword picture until drawn.
- **XP per action from the ledger's balance preview** (owner 2026-11-18, the system adjusts XP): alone, the copper ladder made the Starter Camp twice as long; with the preview's numbers it is 65 minutes against 59 today. Its other proposal, the Copper Helmet, Shield and Chestplate down to Smithing 6, was left out: it breaks the base set's places in the tier. ⚠️ The Starter Camp plan lists those three; the camp is 65 minutes against its 30-minute budget, as it was before.
- **Later, with what uses them**: Nails and Gears (Construction to 99), Arrowheads (Fletching), Ring Bands and Chains (Crafting), Harpoons (Fishing), Kunai (the ammo rule), Plate Gloves and Boots (their slots), the Master Hammer (no rule for what it does yet). Bows and Wands stay at the Blacksmith until Fletching and Enchanting take them.
- **Silver blades** have iron's numbers; the "strong against undead" idea has no rule yet.

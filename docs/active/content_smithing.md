# Content roadmap: Smithing

*Part of session 3 of [concept_content_roadmap.md](concept_content_roadmap.md). Planning agent's proposal, 2026-11-04, revised after the owner's answers, for the owner to edit. Names and rough levels are decided here; quantities, times, damage and prices are the ledger's. Generated and checked by a script: levels 1 to 99 with no gaps, each pick before the metal it mines.*

## The owner's rulings (2026-11-04)

- **99 recipes, one per level**: "Level 1: ingot. Level 2: dagger, Level 3: woodaxe" (brief 480), with improved versions at later metals ("iron dagger at level 22").
- **"Some base items will be available in every kind, such as chestplate and pickaxe. Others will be metal specific (a Cleaver may only be Iron)."**
- **Not items: Sickle, Greatsword, Fittings.**
- **Silver and gold don't get weapon sets**: "they will smith some components, perhaps some silver weapons."
- **Bows and arrows move to Fletching; Smithing makes arrowheads. Wands move to Enchanting.** Those two are planned next.
- **Bands from Smithing, gems set by Crafting.**
- **Gloves and boots get hero slots** (T-303).
- **No Steel. Plan out possible weapons, and cut them later**, including cross-skill ones (below).

## The shape

- **Base items on copper, iron, mythril, adamantine and darkmetal**: Ingot, Dagger, Woodaxe, Pickaxe, Sword, Helmet, Chestplate, Gloves, Boots, Arrowheads, Chisel.
- **Metal-specific items** take the remaining levels; iron, the longest rung, has the most.
- **Silver and gold** are component metals, feeding Crafting, Enchanting, Faith and Summoning.
- **Handles use that rung's wood**, so Forestry and Smithing climb together.
- **Rules kept**: ingots at the Furnace, everything else at the Smithy; better stations are faster, not new lists; coal is the fuel.

## Art

**38 have, 37 adapt, 24 new.** You have every ingot, pick, woodaxe and chisel, five plates, two swords, the iron cleaver (`battleaxe_iron`), and steel gloves, boots and helmet, used here for iron. New shapes include the Dagger, Shield, Arrowheads, Kunai, Mace, Spear, Rapier and the silver and gold components. Most other entries recolour those, using `color_mythril`, `color_addy` and `color_darkmetal`. `battleaxe_copper` and `glove_leather`, `boot_leather`, `boot_slipper` wait for other skills.

## Moved or changed from today

- **Retire**: Copper and Iron Sickles (⚠️ anything needing a sickle must stop), Greatswords, Copper Cleaver, Bows and Wands from the Smithy. ⚠️ Combat lists the Greatsword as Crush; the Mace takes over, and today's content packs change.
- **Iron gear moves earlier**: Iron Sword 38 becomes 25, Iron Chestplate 43 becomes 33.
- **Ingots move to the ladder**: Silver 30 becomes 38, Gold 45 becomes 62, Mythril 60 becomes 50, Adamantine 75 becomes 74, Darkmetal 90 becomes 86.
- **Mythril, Adamantine and Darkmetal gain their sets**; only their picks exist today.
- ⚠️ **Thrown weapons used as ammo are new for Stealth and Melee**: only arrows work that way today, and combat v2 set aside Throwing Daggers. Amends [concept_combat_v2.md](concept_combat_v2.md) if kept.

## Open links

- **Fletching**: bows and arrows, using Arrowheads. **Enchanting**: wands, using Wire, Dust and more. Both planned next.
- **Crafting**: gems set into 24 pieces of jewellery; uses for Chain, Nails, Hinges, Hoops, Fish Hooks and Thread.
- **Faith, Summoning, Science**: their stubs here.
- **Gloves and boots** (T-303): slots, numbers and the hero sheet. Leather and cloth versions come with Crafting.
- **Farming**: no sickle, so check what gates its harvests.
- **The ledger**: 99 recipes means an XP curve to fit, as for Cooking.

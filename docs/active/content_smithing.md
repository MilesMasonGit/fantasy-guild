# Content roadmap: Smithing

*Part of session 3 of [concept_content_roadmap.md](concept_content_roadmap.md). Planning agent's proposal, 2026-11-04, for the owner to edit. Names and rough levels are decided here; quantities, times, damage and prices are the ledger's. Generated and checked by a script: levels 1 to 99 with no gaps, each pick before the metal it mines.*

## The owner's rulings (2026-11-04)

- **99 recipes, one per level.**
- **"We'll likely have more improved versions of items (copper dagger at level 2, iron dagger at level 22)."** Each metal is a tailored set, not an endless list of new kinds.
- **Level 1 ingot, level 2 dagger, level 3 woodaxe** (brief 480).

## The shape

- **One set per metal**, at each rung the ladder gives: Dagger, Sword, Cleaver, Greatsword, Shield, Helmet, Chestplate; plus Woodaxe, Pickaxe, Sickle and Chisel on the five tool metals.
- **Silver and gold make no tools** (ladder ruling): they make Chain, Ring and Amulet Bands, and a Goblet.
- **Iron is the longest rung (23 levels)**: it opens with building materials, then the set from 22, then a short **Steel** run, using your steel art.
- **Handles use that rung's wood**, so Forestry and Smithing climb together.
- **Rules kept**: ingots at the Furnace, everything else at the Smithy; better stations are faster, not new lists; coal is the fuel.

## Art

**38 have, 47 adapt, 14 new.** You have every ingot, pick, woodaxe and chisel, five plates, two swords and two battleaxes. **The new drawings are one of each shape** (Dagger, Greatsword, Shield, Helmet, Sickle, Fittings, Hinges, Hoops, Pot, bands, and two final pieces); most other entries are a recolour of those, using `color_mythril`, `color_addy` and `color_darkmetal`. Today Copper Sickle wears a chisel, and Dagger and Greatsword wear the longsword, as placeholders. `chisel_*` here means a real Chisel.

## Moved or changed from today

- **Iron gear moves earlier**: Iron Sword 38 becomes 25, Iron Chestplate 43 becomes 31.
- **The ingots move to the ladder**: Silver 30 becomes 38, Gold 45 becomes 62, Mythril 60 becomes 50, Adamantine 75 becomes 74, Darkmetal 90 becomes 86.
- **Copper Woodaxe needs ingots and wood**, not raw ore; **Copper Pickaxe** comes at 4.
- **Mythril, Adamantine and Darkmetal gain the full set**; only their picks exist today.
- **The Iron Smithy and Iron Furnace recipes stay Construction's** (wood and buildings session).

## Open links

- **Bows and Wands** are not in this list (a question for the owner).
- **Jewellery**: Ring and Amulet Bands assume Crafting sets the gems.
- **Gloves and boots**: no slot today.
- **Crafting**: Chain, Hinges, Hoops, Nails and Chisels need uses in its session; Construction likewise.
- **Enemies**: which weapons have an effect or a named damage type ("Ember", "Frost").
- **The ledger**: 99 recipes means an XP curve to fit, as for Cooking.

# Effect badges on the Token card

*Owner interview, 2026-11-11, after the Stars vs Buildings picks and the effect
names ([star_ideas_review.md](star_ideas_review.md),
[UI_STYLE "Effect names"](../reference/UI_STYLE.md#effect-names-owner-2026-11-11)).
Many sources (Hall stars, buildings in reach, a hero's gear and drinks) boost
the same few effects; the card shows the totals at a glance and where each
one comes from.*

## The badge row

- **Each badge**: a small icon and the summed total, such as `[icon] +18%`.
- **Where**: on the Token card, **right under the stats line** (work and cycle
  cells, or an enemy's combat line), so the boosts read beside the numbers they
  change.
- **Which effects**: only the **named effects** in UI_STYLE's list. A new common
  effect is named there first, then gets a badge.
- **Only what's working**: an effect at zero, or one that can't apply (Lucky on
  a Token with no rare table), shows nothing. A Token with no boosts has no row.
- **Fixed order**: the UI_STYLE list's order, so a badge sits in the same spot
  on every card. The Hero Effects badge comes last.
- **Held at a limit**: the number turns **gold** (as today's floor note does)
  and the hover says how much counts, such as "held at the limit: +40% of +52%
  counts".
- **Every Token kind**: gathering, work and stations, and **enemies** (one rule
  everywhere, owner 2026-11-11). An enemy's row sits under its combat line.
- **Card only**: nothing new on the mat; the Encyclopedia keeps showing base
  values.

## The hover

One line per source with its amount, then the total:

> Hall star Deep Soil III +3%
> Forge nearby +15%
> Mira's gloves +5%
> **Total +23%**

plus the limit note when one applies.

## What it replaces

- **"Boosted by" goes**: the badges' hovers name each building, so the list of
  buildings in reach and their green effect lines leaves the card.
- A building's own **"Boosts"** list (what it gives the Tokens near it) stays.

## Heroes

- **A working hero's boosts count**: while a hero works the Token, the badge
  totals include their gear and drinks, and the hover names them.
- **A Hero Effects badge** joins the row (last), the hero's portrait as its
  icon. Its hover lists what that hero carries that's working there: ammo kept
  6%, Food heals +10%, a drink's Speed.
- **Carried items wear their own badge**: in the Hero Inspection Drawer
  ([concept_inspection_drawers.md](concept_inspection_drawers.md)), the ammo,
  Food, drinks or bait in a slot wear a small Preservation badge with its
  total; the hover gives the sources.

## Item badges

Effects on one item sit on that item's line, not in the row: **Double Item** on
a Makes or Drops line, **Efficiency** on a recipe input. Same badge, smaller,
at the right end of the line (`[icon] 8%`), with the same hover.

## Rare finds

A Token's rare drop table ([brief 590](briefs/590_new_effects.md)) shows as its
own **Rare finds** group under Drops, with its chance (`Rare finds 1.1%`) in the
twinkle colour, and the Lucky badge on its heading. **Clicking it opens a nested
list** of the table's possible drops, each with its odds; clicking again closes
it.

## Icons

Placeholders first: existing icons that fit (a clock for Speed, an x2 for
Double Loot). Each badge icon goes on the owner's art list, and the owner's
drawings swap in as they're made.

# Content roadmap: Enchanting

*Part of session 7 of [concept_content_roadmap.md](concept_content_roadmap.md), brought forward by the owner. Planning agent's list from the owner's answers, 2026-11-04. Names and rough levels are decided here; damage, quantities, times and prices are the ledger's.*

## The owner's rulings (2026-11-04)

- **Enchanting makes "tiered Wands and Staffs, as well as enchanting jewelry (made by crafting) and magic gear (made by Tailor)."** Space stays open for "other special weapons (such as a Flame Sword for Melee heroes)".
- **"Five tiers, but each one uses a mix of wood. Cedar and Ebony might be needed to craft the highest tier, for example. First tier should just use Oak wood, so it can be made early on."**
- **"Perhaps Staffs have group of types (Fire Staff, Ice Staff) but wands don't. Wands can have their elemental damage modified by Tomes held in the off hand. Sort of like Bow/Arrows, but easier to mix and match."**
- **"Basic mote/energy items that are used in bulk for crafting, possibly as ammo for magic too."**

## The shape

- **Five tiers at 1, 20, 40, 60, 80**, each with a Mote, a Wand, and Fire, Ice and Electric Staffs.
- **Tomes** turn a Wand's damage to their type: three at the start, then Poison, Dark and Light at later tiers. One Wand and several Tomes lets a mage change type cheaply; a Staff is a dedicated weapon of one type.
- **Wood**: Oak at tier 1, then a tier's main wood with Birch or Cedar, so those two have a permanent job.
- **55 recipes, 44 levels open**: for enchanting Tailor's magic gear, special weapons like a Flame Sword, and magical arrows.

## Planning agent's calls

- **Names are suggestions**: Novice, Adept, Expert, Master, Grandmaster.
- **Gems set a Staff's type from tier 2**: Ruby Fire, Sapphire Ice, Topaz Electric. Tier 1 uses only Oak and Motes, so it can be made early.
- **The six gems' enchanted jewellery** follows Smithing's bands: silver from 43, gold from 63.
- **Each piece has one job**: a bonus. No piece here without one; the ledger sets what.

## Moved or changed from today

- **Copper Wand and Iron Wand retire** for Novice and Adept Wands; they leave the Smithy, when this lands. ⚠️ They set Magic's damage type today (Fire, Ice), so Magic heroes need a Tome.
- ⚠️ **New mechanisms**: a Wand with no type of its own, an off-hand Tome that sets it, and **Motes as ammo, if kept**. Combat v2 set aside Motes; this amends [concept_combat_v2.md](concept_combat_v2.md).
- ⚠️ **Enchanting has no station or loop today** beyond an old sketch of a table with three slots ([concept_skill_loops.md](concept_skill_loops.md)); it needs its own design.

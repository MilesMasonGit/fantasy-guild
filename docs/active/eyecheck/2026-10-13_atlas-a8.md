## Decision: the "safe first fight" (step 8)

A new Recruit holds no combat skill and the engine refuses to let a hero
without one fight, so step 8 cannot be completed in a real new game today.
Built as "win any fight". Options, recommendation first:

- **A.** Keep the step; ticket "Recruits get a weak basic attack" (engine
  change, concept_combat's "everyone fights"); you author a weak, safe enemy
  in the Starter Camp.
- **B.** Drop the fight step from the chain until everyone can fight.
- **C.** Add a step before it: promote a hero at the Fighter's Academy
  (works today, but long and needs Stone).
- **D.** The step spawns its own trivial enemy beside the Hall (new code and
  a new Token).

Decision (owner, 2026-10-13, in chat): **A**. Ticket T-167 filed.

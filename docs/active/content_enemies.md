# Enemies: the roster up the tiers

*Owner interview 2026-11-25 (planning agent, standing in for the content agent): the "enemies
session" the content roadmap has waited on since 4 Nov. The content agent turns this into catalogue
entries and the CMS pack (T-663 for steal tables, T-706 for the roster). Levels, stats, drop
amounts and chances are the ledger's; enemy kinds and styles follow brief 740.*

## Rulings (owner 2026-11-25)

1. **Three regular camps and one boss per tier** above Copper: Iron, Silver, Mythril, Gold,
   Adamantine, Darkmetal. Copper keeps today's 20 enemies.
2. **By biome**: each camp belongs to biomes; further rings hold the higher tiers.
3. **Stranger as you climb**: familiar early, magical in the middle, fantastical late.
4. **The Elementals stay early** and live on as Summoning's Summoned versions.
5. Every regular camp gets a **steal table** (Crime, [content_crime.md](content_crime.md)); bosses
   none.

## Jobs the drops fill

- **Already waiting**: Silk (Tailor's fabrics), Blue Scale (Crafting's Drake armour), Red Scale
  (Dragonscale), Raw Chicken and Eggs (Cooking's held dishes), Feathers (Fletching, Alchemy),
  Pearls and Seaweed, Hide, Ore Prospecting Maps (brief 860), Coins (brief 870), Motes.
- **New materials, each needing a job or cut** (no clutter): Spider Fang, Harpy Talon, Ectoplasm,
  Heartwood, Naga Scale, Griffin Feather, Salamander Skin, Minotaur Horn, Troll Hide, Basilisk Eye,
  Bone Dust, Kraken Ink, Phoenix Feather, Void Shard, Dragon Heart, Ash. These are meant first for
  **Alchemy's late potions**, which wait on "enemy drops from the enemy session"; then Enchanting's
  special weapons and Tailor's top tiers. The content agent matches each one or cuts it.
- Drake Meat and Dragon Meat: Cooking's late dishes, or cut.

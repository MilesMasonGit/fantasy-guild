# Summoning: the summons

*Owner interview 2026-11-25 (planning agent, standing in for the content agent). The mechanic is
[concept_skill_identities.md](concept_skill_identities.md) "Summoning" and brief 810 K5. This page
holds the content rulings and the first list; the content agent turns it into catalogue entries and
a change pack (T-663). Numbers (charges, costs, cycle times) are the ledger's.*

## Rulings (owner 2026-11-25)

1. **One summon per main tier**: five summons, at Summoning **1, 20, 40, 60, 80**, each stronger than
   the last. Levels between stay open.
2. **Gathering only**: summons work Mining, Forestry, Fishing or Farming, never a making skill, so
   heroes still make everything.
3. **One skill each**, at a fixed level.
4. **Each summon is a "Summoned" version of its enemy** (owner): named "Summoned Water Elemental" and so on, no new names. Recolours of the Elementals and Slimes (Thorn, Water, Wind and Fire Elementals; Small and
   Greater Slime): the magical enemies, matched to a job.

*Planning agent's calls (say if any is wrong):* a summon's skill level sits ten above its unlock, so
it is worth calling the moment it unlocks; the Circle spends something the copied enemy drops plus
one Mote of a fitting kind; Mining appears twice (at 40 and 80) because four skills share five tiers
and veins are the biggest bulk need. Motes come from Enchanting's Leylines, so Summoning leans on
Enchanting from its first summon.

## Art

Five recolours (A, adapt): each summon is its enemy's sprite in a new palette, with a faint glow so
it never reads as an enemy. The Summoning Circle is a placeholder building (T-663).

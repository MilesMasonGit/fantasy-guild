# Combat styles: what each one is for

*From the Systems Audit, A15 Melee ([card](audit/A15_melee.md)), owner
interview 2026-11-20. Melee is written here; Ranged, Magic and Stealth are
filled in by their own audit sessions (A16 to A18), then the planning agent
finishes it with the owner. Tickets already filed: T-565 to T-571 (Melee), T-572 to T-576 (Ranged), T-577 to T-582 (Magic), T-583 to T-588 (Stealth). All four styles are now written.*

## The idea

Each combat style is a different way to play, not just a different side of
the matchup cycle. The styles differ in **what they need** to work, **how
steady** their damage is, and **how far gear can specialise** them.

## Melee: the steady, self-sufficient style

In the owner's words (2026-11-20): "Melee's main appeal is that it is
consistent damage and defense without needing a lot of support. Other styles
require ammo like arrows, niche utility items for magic, or are more wild and
inconsistent. Melee can be performed with weapons and armor that don't break
and can all be crafted from the same base metal material, making it a
reliable beginner combat style."

- **Needs nothing else**: no ammo, no consumable kit. Weapons and armour come
  from one metal line (Smithing) and last.
- **Steady**: its steadiness comes from that gear and from small weapon
  traits, not from a special Melee rule in the fight (no built-in miss or
  damage bonus).
- **Defends well**: metal armour and shields cost Melee nothing (they cut
  Magic and Stealth damage today).
- **The beginner style**: barehanded is Melee, and a copper sword is enough
  to start.
- **Weapon kinds**: every kind (sword, cleaver, later more) carries a small
  trait that fits it (T-565). Two hands out-damage one hand of the same metal,
  paying for the lost shield (T-568); the rule waits for the first two-handed
  weapon (owner 2026-11-21).
- **Levels by metal**: a weapon asks a level in its style by its metal tier,
  on the metals' ladder (copper 1, iron 20, and so on; owner 2026-11-21,
  T-566).

## Magic: specialised through many items (owner's first note)

"Combat styles like Magic can be specialized through equipment, to be more
efficient by taking advantages of weaknesses and elemental damage, but it
requires a more diverse collection of items, and it takes effort to get all
of those."

*From A17 Magic ([card](audit/A17_magic.md)), owner 2026-11-20.*

- **Pick the element for the enemy**: a mage brings the element the enemy is
  weak to; a resisted element is wasted.
- **A kit to collect**: the most items of any style, and the most room to
  specialise: wands, staffs and tomes are its weapon kinds (like swords and
  maces), each tier's Wand and Tome taking its Mote's element (T-577, owner
  2026-11-21); Tailor's mage clothes (T-578).
- **Spends Motes**: every cast spends one Mote, the kind its weapon names;
  with none left the hero casts at half damage (T-579). Motes come from
  Leylines and also go into crafting.
- **Defends lightly**: metal cuts Magic damage; leather and cloth don't
  (T-580).
- Not a glass cannon: the owner moved that feel to Stealth.

## Ranged

*From A16 Ranged ([card](audit/A16_ranged.md)), owner 2026-11-20.*

- **Hits harder, paid in arrows**: a bow and arrow out-damage a sword of the
  same metal; arrows are the running cost, spent hit or miss. When the last
  one is shot the hero leaves the fight and says so (T-573).
- **Arrows change the shot**: many arrow kinds, each a different part (types,
  Sunder, later poison and magic); the player picks the arrow for the enemy.
- **Two damage types a shot**, mostly through arrows and a few special bows:
  plain bows add Stab, some special bows carry an element.
- **Saving arrows** is its gear's specialisation: ranger armour or trinkets,
  Ranged perks and a Hall star give Preservation (T-574).
- **Fletching makes bows and arrows** (T-572, by
  [content_fletching.md](content_fletching.md)).

## Stealth

*From A18 Stealth ([card](audit/A18_stealth.md)), owner 2026-11-20.*

- **The wild glass cannon**: plain hits weaker than a sword's, crits often
  and big; on average equal, with a wide spread (T-585).
- **Crits often, not bigger** (owner 2026-11-21): no Crit Damage effect; a
  crit is double for everyone. Stealth's spread comes from a high crit chance
  on gear, traits and perks, up to a ceiling of 75% (T-584; T-583 closed).
- **Fragile**: its clothes trade defence for crit and Dodge
  (T-578); metal cuts Stealth damage.
- **Sometimes spends**: daggers need nothing; Kunai are thrown and spend
  themselves, the last one kept for half damage (T-586).
- **Poisons**: optional consumables, taken like an elixir with a Stealth
  weapon, each its own buff for a set number of attacks; leftover attacks carry into
  later fights (T-587, owner 2026-11-21).
- The opening strike stays dropped (owner, 2026-10-30).

## Shared by all four

- The matchup cycle (melee > ranged > magic > stealth > melee) stays; enemy
  styles are spread so every hero style meets good and bad matchups (T-567).
- Each style's text on the hero sheet says what the style is about and the
  weapons that exist, promising nothing (T-569).
- The weapon tier table (damage by metal and speed, level by tier, a
  two-hand line) is the content agent's authoring guide in
  CONTENT_PHILOSOPHY, not code (T-568).

## Each style's signature (owner, 2026-11-21)

What its late perks (T-522), the Hall's combat stars and its gear push it
toward:

| Style | Signature |
|---|---|
| Melee | Defence and steadiness: armour, Accuracy, resistances |
| Ranged | Saving arrows (Preservation) and the arrow's damage types |
| Magic | Hitting weaknesses: Type Damage by element |
| Stealth | Crit chance (to the 75% ceiling) and Dodge |

## Weapon traits (owner, 2026-11-21; the content agent sets the numbers, T-565)

| Kind | Trait |
|---|---|
| Sword | +5 Accuracy (steady) |
| Cleaver | more Damage, slower; keeps Double Raw Beef |
| Mace | hits Crush, +10% Crush damage, slower (content agent's call, kept by the owner 2026-11-21) |
| Bow | +5 Accuracy (as today) |
| Dagger | +5 Crit chance (as today) |
| Kunai | +5 Crit chance, thrown |
| Wand | Attack Speed +10% (quick casts) |
| Staff | Type Damage +10% of its element, slower |
| Tome | resistance to its own element |

## The style texts on the hero sheet (owner, 2026-11-21, T-569)

One line each; the owner may edit them at the eye-check.

- **Melee**: Steady damage and strong defence. Swords and cleavers from one metal, no ammo. The beginner's style.
- **Ranged**: Hits hard from afar, paid for in arrows. Pick the arrow for the enemy.
- **Magic**: Strikes an enemy's weakness with the right element. Wands, staffs and tomes, each burning Motes.
- **Stealth**: Wild and fragile: weaker hits, frequent crits. Daggers, thrown Kunai and optional poisons.

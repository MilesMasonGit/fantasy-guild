# A living game: events, the return, hero identity

*D7 of the post-crunch plan ([ideas.md](ideas.md)). Owner interview, started
2026-10-09. **Done** (owner closed it 2026-10-09). Builds on D1's "pops of activity" and tone
([concept_tone_and_world.md](concept_tone_and_world.md)).*

## Events

- **A few per hour of play**: roughly every 15–30 minutes something small,
  with rarer big ones.
- **Treats, with optional risk**: events offer something good; any danger is
  the player's choice (accepting a request that leads to a fight). Nothing bad
  happens to a guild just for being away.
- **Kinds**:
  - **things that land**: a shooting star to mine, a meteorite, a mushroom
    ring; a temporary special node;
  - **visitors with dialogue**: the frog prince, a travelling merchant, a lost
    knight;
  - **Region happenings** tied to the map: a typhoon on the coast, a bloom in
    the meadow (the Atlas concept's environmental events).
- **Offline: landed nodes yes, visitors wait.** Heroes work landed nodes
  offline as normal; visitors who need a choice wait at the Hall until the
  player returns. No missed conversations.
- **Where: partly per Region type.** Happenings follow the map; landings and
  visitors can come anywhere, some tied to biomes or later planes (stranger
  visitors deeper in).

## Visitors and dialogue

- **Dialogue in speech bubbles on the mat**: the visitor speaks in bubbles;
  the player's replies appear as small buttons under them. A few lines and a
  choice or two (D1).
- **Requests become quest Tokens** beside the Hall (the existing quest system):
  "Bring 20 Fish to the Frog Prince", "Defeat the Bog Witch". Heroes do them
  through normal work; the visitor rewards on completion.
- **A recurring cast plus rare one-offs**: familiar visitors return with new
  requests (the frog prince's saga over several visits).

## The return

- **The "While you were away" summary is a plain report.** The main focus is
  **items and XP gained**, plus a small section of **notable events** (random
  events, a hero defeated) and a section with **numbers of enemies defeated**.
  (This replaces the "feel like a present" framing in ideas.md.)
- **Nothing extra after it closes**: the board's normal pops are enough.

## Hero identity

- **Recolouring heroes**: preset colour schemes, plus full recolouring of key
  parts of the clothing and the skin tone. Available **from the beginning**;
  each new hero is generated with a random preset.
- **Names** follow D1 (homely, classic, epithets; ordinary names rarer);
  players rename freely.
- **Any quirks or personality are flavour only**: no effect on play, so there's
  no reason to reroll heroes and no balance work.

## Effects through bubbles

Settled in D1's "pops of activity" (quiet pops for rules firing, every time).

# Science: mastery tracks

*Owner interview 2026-11-25 (planning agent, standing in for the content agent). The mechanic is
[concept_skill_identities.md](concept_skill_identities.md) "Science" and brief 810 K6. The content
agent turns this into the CMS pack (T-663). Effect sizes and point thresholds are the ledger's,
inside mastery's share of the boost budget (BALANCE_RULES section 5).*

## Rulings (owner 2026-11-25)

1. **Every gathering node kind gets a track**: each vein, tree, fruit tree, bush, crop and Fishing
   Spot kind.
2. **Ranks give the common effects** (Speed, Yield, Preservation, Lucky, Refresh, Double Item, Growth),
   in an order varied per kind.
3. **Five ranks** per track.
4. **Laboratory variants reach higher-level nodes; a University** support building speeds Science
   up in its reach.

## The tracks

One pattern per kind of node, varied so the skills don't all read the same (planning agent's
call; the content agent may vary single kinds within them):

| Node kinds | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 | Rank 4 | Rank 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Veins and deposits (Copper to Darkmetal, Coal, Clay, Quartz, Stone, Marble, Basalt) | Speed | Lucky | Yield | Refresh | Double Item |
| Trees (Oak to Ebony) | Speed | Yield | Refresh | Lucky | Double Item |
| Fruit trees and bushes (Apple to Coconut, Redberry to Pineapple, Grapevine, Watermelon) | Growth | Yield | Speed | Lucky | Double Item |
| Field crops (Wheat and the crops to come) | Growth | Speed | Yield | Preservation (seed) | Double Item |
| Fishing Spot kinds | Speed | Preservation (Bait) | Lucky | Yield | Double Item |

A kind can be studied from the node's own gathering level in Science (Copper at 1, Iron at 20,
Ebony at 80); once unlocked it can reach full mastery.

*Planning agent's calls:* event nodes that land and leave (Geode, Fallen Star, Fish Shoal…) have no
track; Stumps and Sprouts count as their tree or crop; Refresh means a lower chance to run out (brief
860 R1).

## The Laboratory and the University

- **Laboratory**: base reach covers nodes up to level 20; variants at Science 20, 40, 60, 80 reach
  higher nodes, each picked with one menu effect (Speed or XP).
- **University**: one support building, Speed for Science in its reach, built on a Wood or Stone
  Foundation like other supports.

# Content roadmap: Tailor

*A new skill (owner, 2026-11-04; T-304). Part of session 7 of [concept_content_roadmap.md](concept_content_roadmap.md). Planning agent's list from the owner's answers. Names and rough levels are decided here; numbers are the ledger's.*

## The owner's rulings (2026-11-04)

- **"Primarily the stealth and magic armor (fine clothes and wizard robes)."**
- **"Crafting will be too crowded with 3 combat skills worth of gear in it."** Ranged gear stays in Crafting, Melee in Smithing.
- **"Various cloth materials that are used in structures."** Sacks and sails included. **Parchment and books are Crafting. No banners.**
- **Three tiers of fabric.**
- **Hat, chest, gloves and boots per tier for each of Magic and Stealth, and "capes as well, we'll have various cloaks that can occupy that slot too."**

## The shape

- **Fabrics**: Linen from Flax (Farming), Wool Cloth from Wool, Silk Cloth from Silk. Tiers 4 and 5 are Silk with Motes, so Enchanting feeds the top of Tailor.
- **String from Flax at level 1**, so the first bow is possible early.
- **Nine pieces per armour tier, at the same place in each**, 20 levels apart, as in Smithing: Magic Hat, Stealth Hood, Gloves, Grips, Slippers, Soft Boots, Robe, Tunic, Cape.
- **56 recipes, 43 levels open**, for what Construction and later skills turn out to need.

## Planning agent's calls

- **Moon and Void are names for tiers 4 and 5**: Silk with Water or Dark Motes. They're suggestions, and keep fabrics at three.
- **Three style cloaks** beside the plain Cape, one each for Ranged, Stealth and Magic, as your "various cloaks". Melee's could come from Enchanting.
- **Structure materials are few**: Canvas, Sack, Sail, Drapes. They keep a place only if the wood and buildings session uses them.

## What this needs

- ⚠️ **A cape slot**: a third new slot, beside gloves and boots (T-303).
- **Sources**: Flax (Farming, a new crop), Wool (a pasture animal, with Taming later), Silk (spiders, from the enemy session).
- **A station**: a Loom or Tailor's Bench (wood and buildings). T-304 covers the skill itself.
- **Crafting keeps** Ranged's leather gear and Parchment; its old notes on cloth armour and String change.
- **Enchanting** has open levels for these pieces.

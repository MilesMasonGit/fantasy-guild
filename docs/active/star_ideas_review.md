# Star upgrade ideas: the owner's review (2026-11-11)

The director brainstormed 117 Star upgrade ideas (three loops, solid and
wild mixed); the owner marked each Approve, Maybe or No on a review page
(https://claude.ai/artifact/MA3s5YoeYwbJWktxinB3Xh) and left notes.
**12 approved, 23 maybe, 82 no.** The owner's notes are quoted or closely
paraphrased; nothing here is designed yet. This page is input for the
planning agent's interviews, not a brief.

## The owner's four takeaways

1. **Families, not one-offs.** Many upgrades work the same way and only
   change their target (a Token kind, a skill, an item category). Design
   each *effect* once, then give it per-target versions.
2. **Show common effects on the Token card.** Effects such as "chance to
   double outputs" and "chance to keep the inputs" may want a UI element:
   probably small hoverable icons on the Token card.
3. **Stars vs Buildings.** Stars are permanent and global, so they are tiny
   incremental steps (about 1 % per rank). Buildings only reach the Tokens in
   their range, so their boosts are the big ones. Which effect belongs where
   still has to be decided, effect by effect.
4. **Events need their own planning session.** Several ideas came back as
   "this is an Event": a special Token that appears for a short while and
   gives buffs or a haul (weather, a wandering merchant, a fallen star).

## The effect families the notes point to

Each family is one effect with per-target versions. Where the owner named a
side (Star or Building), it is noted; the rest is open (takeaway 3).

| Family | What it does | Targets the owner named | From ideas |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Keep the inputs** (preservation) | Chance a cycle does not use up its materials | a skill; ammo; consumables (work drinks, Food); fishing bait and harpoons | Thrifty Hands ✅, Arrow Recovery ✅, Strong Drink, Larder Keeper, Heavy Catches |
| **Double the outputs** | Chance a cycle's output comes out doubled | one item or category per Star pick; enemy loot | Double Batch ✅, Spoils of War ✅ |
| **Keep a charge** | Chance a cycle does not spend one of the Token's charges | Fields, Fruit Trees, Wood Trees, Ore Veins, etc., one version each | Deep Soil ✅ |
| **More charges** | A Token kind holds more charges; fairly high level | types of Tokens | Gentle Hands |
| **Rare drop tables** | Every gathering Token kind has a rare-drop chance that rolls on its own table (a Vein's Gem Drop); the upgrade raises that kind's chance | per Token kind; fishing could have a second special drop | Lucky Finds ✅, Treasure in the Water |
| **Fewer inputs** | A recipe needs fewer of an input; possibly seeds bought in bulk (10–15 per sowing) with the quantity reduced | owner leans Building ("quite powerful") | Cheaper Recipes, Seed Saver |
| **XP for everyone** | A one-time flat XP grant to every current and future hero, so new heroes start above level 1 and nothing is wasted | all heroes | First Make, Fresh Recruits |
| **Combat sets** | Sets of combat buffs: damage, speed or a stat per style | combat styles and stats | Style Mastery, Quick Hands, Drill Yard |
| **Hall well sets** | Hall productions in sets of items; upgrading the Star raises the rate of the whole set | item sets | New Wells |

## Approved (12)

- **Lucky Finds**: rare drop tables per Token kind (see the family above).
- **Deep Soil**: chance not to spend a charge, one version per Token kind.
- **Giant Crop**: a tiny chance a harvest comes in giant, ten times the
  yield, with a small celebration on the mat.
- **Thrifty Hands**: a skill's chance to keep its materials.
- **Double Batch**: one item or category gets a chance to come out doubled.
- **Long-lasting Brews**: work potions and drinks last longer.
- **Bigger Packs**: one more loadout slot of a chosen kind (Food, Potion,
  Trinket, Ammo), one Star per kind, as Larder and Trinket Case do today.
- **Hearty Meals**: Food heals more in a fight.
- **Field Medic**: heroes regain HP faster out of a fight.
- **Spoils of War**: enemies have a chance to drop double loot.
- **Arrow Recovery**: ammo has a chance not to be used up.
- **Neighbours**: buildings of skills that feed each other (a Farmstead by a
  Kitchen) work faster in each other's reach.

## Maybe (23), with the owner's notes

**Folded into a family above:** Heavy Catches (no special fish exist; the
hero fishes for one kind; becomes "keep the bait or harpoons"), Seed Saver,
Gentle Hands, Cheaper Recipes, First Make (not as written; becomes XP for
everyone), Fresh Recruits (same), Strong Drink, Larder Keeper, Treasure in
the Water, Style Mastery, Drill Yard, Quick Hands, New Wells.

**Events (for the Events session):** Weather Lore (weather as Events that
spawn a short-lived buff Token; Stars could make Events more frequent),
Wandering Merchant, Fallen Star.

**Building ideas:**
- **Shared Pot**: a tavern-like building built near fights, giving cooking
  stations in its reach a set buff on each completion (say 1 HP per cycle).
- **Compost**: a composter building that eats scrap items to speed Fields.

**Other notes:**
- **Fertile Ground** (changing the mat itself): interesting, but unclear how
  it fits the mechanics, and it may be too late in development.
- **Leyline Bloom**: maybe different kinds of Leylines boost different skills.
- **Scouts**: what a place holds should show by default in the Yields panel,
  not behind a Star (a possible ticket).
- **Bigger Vault**: the game already has a way to raise Bank storage.
- **Pat the Limes**: it would be cute if Limes joined the heroes on their
  journey and wandered the mat; a buff from him would be interesting.

## Planning sessions this needs

1. **Stars vs Buildings**: for each family, is it a Star (tiny, global,
   ranked) or a Building effect (big, in reach), or both at different
   sizes; and the rank sizes.
2. **Effect icons on the Token card**: which effects get an icon, what the
   hover says, how several stack.
3. **Events**: what an Event is (a short-lived special Token), how often,
   what it gives, and how Stars tilt them. Weather, the merchant and the
   fallen star are the first examples.
4. **Limes on the journey** (smaller): Limes following the guild and
   wandering the mat, maybe with a buff.

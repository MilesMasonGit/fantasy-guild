## The tutorial

- **The cartographer's quests, one at a time**: today's step-by-step quest
  Tokens, spoken by the cartographer in his eerie, muddled voice
  ([concept_tone_and_world.md](concept_tone_and_world.md)). Runs in the
  Starter Camp, about 30–60 minutes.
- **It teaches**: heroes, flags and gathering (recruit, plant a flag, gather,
  collect loot); building and crafting (a Foundation, a station, a recipe); a
  first fight (a safe first enemy; everyone fights); and the Atlas (the endgame
  sites' tease, then the first maps and writing the first world).
- ⚠️ Today's 11-step tutorial teaches the pre-Atlas opening (buy an Oak Forest
  from the Shop, build a Workbench); it's rewritten after brief 70.

## After the tutorial

- **The cartographer gives no more quests**; he speaks only now and then (loop
  hints, the ritual). This narrows D1's "quest giver" to the tutorial.
- **Ongoing quests**: **Notice Board bounties** and **visitor requests** (D7).
- **Bounties**: collect, defeat and craft, **scaled to progress** (asking for
  things from the guild's current chains, not always basics).
- **Rewards**: **maps and modifiers** (a reliable map source, as the Atlas
  plans) and **materials**.

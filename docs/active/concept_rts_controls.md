# Select and right-click: RTS-style controls

*Owner interview, 2026-11-11. A second way to give orders, beside drag and
drop: click to select a hero or a movable Token, right-click the mat to send
it. Both controls work at the same time and follow the same rules.*

## Selecting

- **Single click selects** a hero (on the mat or on the hero bar) or a
  **movable Token** (one you could drag today). One thing at a time.
- **Selected = a gold outline**: on the mat, and on the hero's hero bar card.
  A selected hero's **job lights** too, as hovering them does today.
- **Double-click inspects**: a Token opens its card, a hero their Hero drawer,
  from the mat or the hero bar. The double-click also selects.
- **Unmovable Tokens** (enemies, fixed landmarks): a single click does
  nothing but clear any selection; a double-click inspects. One rule
  everywhere: single click selects, double-click inspects.
- **Clearing**: a click on blank mat, Escape, or selecting something else. It
  also clears when leaving the mat (the World Map, the Guild Hall, travelling)
  or switching to a map mode such as Demolish. Opening the Bank or the Shop
  keeps it, since the mat stays underneath.
- **An open panel closes** when you single-click something else: a Token card
  or the Hero drawer closes, and the new pick is selected.
- **The selection stays after an order**, so a second right-click corrects the
  first.

## Right-click orders

Orders land **on the mat only**; Bank and Shop panels over the mat ignore a
right-click as an order. Hero bar cards can be selected on any screen.

**A selected hero**: a right-click does exactly what dropping them there does:
- work the Token, fight the enemy, hunt from an enemy camp;
- go home on the Guild Hall;
- walk to bare ground and wait, the flag marking the spot.

Refusals are the drag's ("Needs Pickaxe", "Mining level too low (needs 25)").

**Stop**: right-clicking a hero onto the Token they already work, or the enemy
they already fight, **stops them**: a job can't be given twice. They wait where
they stand with the yellow "needs a job" mark. The same right-click with
**nothing selected**, on a working hero or the Token they work, also stops
them. This replaces today's right-click send-home; home is now a right-click
on the Guild Hall, or a drag there.

**A selected Token**: a right-click on the mat sends it there, with the same
placement checks as a drop. A spot it can't take is **refused with the drag's
reason**, and the Token stays put. It travels in a **short arc, like flying
loot** (about half a second), and lands with a small settle. A hero working it
walks after it, as after a drag.

## While something is selected

The pointer shows **the same hints as a drag**, so you see the result before
you right-click:
- **For a hero**: Tokens they can work glow; the drag hints show over the Token
  under the pointer; a dashed gold line runs from the hero to the pointer.
- **For a Token**: its ghost and reach ring at the pointer.

## Not now

- Selecting several at once (shift-click, box select): a later ticket if
  wanted.
- Orders on the World Map (sending a selected hero to a Region).

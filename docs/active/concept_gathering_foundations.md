# Gathering foundations: one per gathering skill

*Owner, 2026-11-24, during the Fishing review (planning agent). Opened, not yet interviewed in full.*

> "Players who want to create new nodes for basic gathering or other essential production will have
> these segregated from each other." (owner)

## The rulings so far (owner 2026-11-24)

1. **Each gathering skill gets its own basic foundation**, from which its nodes and specialty
   buildings are built:
   - **Mining: Excavation**
   - **Forestry: Nursery**
   - **Fishing: Dock**
   - **Farming: Farmland**
2. **Crafting and the other skills keep today's Wood and Stone Foundations** for their buildings.
3. **The Dock** stands **on the shore, touching water** (the "Stands on" terrain rule), is **its own
   Token**, and offers: the **Fishing Hut** (moved onto it, with its variants), a **Bait Shed** (turns
   Raw Shrimp into Bait) and a **Lure Workshop** (makes lures).

4. **Excavation** digs a new vein, built with an **Ore Prospecting Map** for that ore (a rare drop
   from mining it or from other sources), any Region, limited only by the Token cap (owner 2026-11-24,
   [content_mining_forestry.md](content_mining_forestry.md) "Mining review").
5. **Nursery** becomes a Forest of a chosen species, paid in that species' seeds (owner 2026-11-24,
   [content_mining_forestry.md](content_mining_forestry.md) "Forestry review").
6. **Farmland** becomes a Field, an Orchard or a Herb Garden (owner 2026-11-24,
   [content_farming.md](content_farming.md) "Farming review").

## Open (to interview)

- How a gathering foundation is got (bought, built by Construction, a Guild Hall star) and what it costs.
- Whether buildings that today sit on Wood or Stone Foundations for these skills (a Mine, a Logging
  Camp, a Field) move onto the new ones.
- Whether the Dock's Bait Shed and Lure Workshop replace the Crafting recipes for Bait and lures, or
  sit beside them ([content_fishing.md](content_fishing.md)).

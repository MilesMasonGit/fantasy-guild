# Gathering foundations: one per gathering skill

*Owner, 2026-11-24, during the Fishing review (planning agent). Opened, not yet interviewed in full.*

> "Players who want to create new nodes for basic gathering or other essential production will have
> these segregated from each other." (owner)

## The rulings so far (owner 2026-11-24)

1. **Each gathering skill gets its own basic foundation**, from which its nodes and specialty
   buildings are built:
   - **Mining: Excavation**
   - **Forestry: Nursery**
   - **Fishing: Dock**
   - **Farming: Farmland**
2. **Crafting and the other skills keep today's Wood and Stone Foundations** for their buildings.
3. **The Dock** stands **on the shore, touching water** (the "Stands on" terrain rule), is **its own
   Token**, and offers: the **Fishing Hut** (moved onto it, with its variants), a **Bait Shed** (turns
   Raw Shrimp into Bait). *The Lure Workshop was dropped 2026-11-24 (point 10).*

4. **Excavation** digs a new vein, built with an **Ore Prospecting Map** for that ore (a rare drop
   from mining it or from other sources), any Region, limited only by the Token cap (owner 2026-11-24,
   [content_mining_forestry.md](content_mining_forestry.md) "Mining review").
5. **Nursery** becomes a Forest of a chosen species, paid in that species' seeds (owner 2026-11-24,
   [content_mining_forestry.md](content_mining_forestry.md) "Forestry review").
6. **Farmland** becomes a Field, an Orchard or a Herb Garden (owner 2026-11-24,
   [content_farming.md](content_farming.md) "Farming review").

## Settled 2026-11-24 (owner)

7. **Got like today's Foundations**: bought the same way, then built by a Construction hero into its
   Token, paying that Token's own cost (seeds for a Forest, a Prospecting Map for a vein, a seed for a
   Field).
8. **Where each stands**: **Excavation on stone**, **Farmland on grass**, **the Dock on the shore**
   (owner); the **Nursery on any land** (planning agent's call, not ruled; say if it should ask forest
   floor or grass).
9. **Sources go on the gathering foundations; Wood and Stone Foundations build the support
   buildings** (owner): veins (Excavation), Forests (Nursery), Fields, Orchards and Herb Gardens
   (Farmland). Mines, Minecarts, Logging Camps, and Farming's Well, Composter, Beehive and Windmill
   are supports, on Wood or Stone. **Fishing works differently**: its sources are spots that come by
   themselves, so the Dock holds Fishing's buildings: the Fishing Hut, the spot building, the Bait
   Shed.
10. **The Bait Shed is the station** where Bait (Fishing recipes) is made. **Lures stay Crafting
    recipes**, made at Crafting's usual stations; **no Lure Workshop** (owner 2026-11-24) ([content_fishing.md](content_fishing.md)).

# A tutorial with a guide: an NPC who walks the player through the basics

*From the detail pass, section 13 (owner, 2026-11-04). Not ready to build: it waits for an interview, together with
the very early game. Replaces the cartographer's quest Tokens in [concept_quests_tutorial.md](concept_quests_tutorial.md).*

## What the owner said

- "Something completely different": **an NPC with special speech bubbles who hand-holds the player through the
  essential mechanics of the game.**
- **The first step will probably show how to assign a hero to a task.**
- **The thing to use next gets a hard gold outline** (recommended and chosen; the owner's note of 2026-10-08).
- **The very early game needs planning of its own**, separately from the detail pass. The tutorial takes the player
  through the basics to get them started, so the two are designed together.

## Open for the interview

- Who is the NPC? The cartographer, as today's words are, or someone new? Do they stand on the mat, at the screen's
  edge or only appear in the bubble? Art needed from the owner.
- What makes the bubbles special, compared with heroes' speech?
- Does the game wait for the player at each step, or can they wander off? Can it be skipped, and can it be replayed?
- Which mechanics are essential, in what order, and how long should it run? Today's is 30 to 60 minutes of 12 steps.
- Do steps give rewards? Today they do, as loot.
- What becomes of the first camp's content once the very early game is planned? See
  [concept_content_roadmap.md](concept_content_roadmap.md).
- Are its words authored in the CMS (T-014)?

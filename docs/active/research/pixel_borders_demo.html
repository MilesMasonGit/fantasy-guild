Pixel borders: five ways to draw one panel

The same hero panel (from UI_STYLE.md) and a tooltip, framed each way. Every pixel frame is one art pixel thick (2 screen pixels, the size of one pixel of a Token on the mat) with a hard shadow 2 art pixels down and right, like a carried Token's.

The buttons zoom the whole page, standing in for Windows display scaling (100 %, 125 %, 150 %, 200 %). At 125 % and 150 % one art pixel is not a whole number of screen pixels: look at which frames keep even corners.

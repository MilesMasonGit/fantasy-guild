Level-up flash: pick one

A hero levels up on a piece of mat, on a loop. The level-up float is the game's own (the skill icon, the old level flashing gold, then rolling to the new one, a signature perk named in gold at 50, 75 and 99). On top of it the reach ring from the mat bursts out once from the hero in gold and breaks up, and a few pixel sparkles fly out. The small flash is for every 5 levels; the big one for 10, 25, 50, 75 and 99. Each loop shows the next level. Pick a letter: D (the loud one the owner asked for), A, B or C.

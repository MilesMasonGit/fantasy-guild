# Brief 90 — The Performance Envelope

The crunch's last step (crunch plan step "Performance Envelope"): write the
game's real limits down as **design rules** content must respect, in the GDD
§14.

**Inputs:** the final `bench:draw`, cost table, engine bench and drag bench
after the Atlas and terrain; brief 60's definition of "smooth".

## E1 — Measure the limits (engineer)

At "perf build, 4× slowdown, smooth": the most Tokens on a Region (the cap),
heroes, enemies at once, loot piles, simultaneous fights, notifications per
minute, and the offline catch-up time at 24 h. Use the stress boards; add
scenarios where needed.

## E2 — Write the rules (director)

GDD §14: each limit as a rule a content author can follow ("a Region holds at
most N Tokens; a Base Map's budget must fit it"; "an enemy camp keeps at most
N enemies alive"…), with the measured number and the date. Owner review.

**Done when:** GDD §14 is filled in and the owner has read it; T-071 (canvas
mat) is decided against those limits.

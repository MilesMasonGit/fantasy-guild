# Token spacing and reach

*Owner interview 2026-11-26 (planning agent), from T-718 and the balancer's method review
([balance_method_review.md](balance_method_review.md)). Why: "The game is a spatial puzzle of
tradeoffs. A building takes up space, naturally limiting the number of tokens in range" (owner), and
the owner's target is **at most 12 Tokens in one reach**. Today 30 fit.*

## Rulings (owner 2026-11-26)

1. **Wider apart, same reach.** Tokens never overlap: the closest two can stand is edges touching,
   **128 u centre to centre** (hitbox 100 %, overlap 0 %). **Reach stays 240 u.** Reach over spacing
   is 1.875, so exactly **12** Tokens fit within reach of one Token.
2. **One reach for everything**: camps, spawners (a Forest or Orchard planting its trees) and every
   "stands near" rule use the one standard reach.
3. **All Tokens are one size.** The half-size "small" Token goes; every Token is drawn and spaced as a
   standard one. The owner draws 64 px art for the 14 small Tokens (seven Stumps, Wheat Sprout, and
   the Wolf, Crab, Bee, Fire Elemental, Sulphur Lizard and Frog); until then their 32 px art is drawn
   at double size.
4. **Start fresh.** Old saves are not carried across this change (the game is unreleased): an older
   save is refused with a plain message, and the player starts a new guild.
5. **A new starting area**, laid out by the director: around the camp, **one clump per gathering
   skill** (Copper and Stone veins; Oak trees; Berry bushes and a Field; a Fishing Spot by water), with
   room near each clump for the first buildings.
6. **The mat and the Token limit stay** (1920 x 1040, 128 Tokens). At the new spacing the mat fills at
   roughly 120 to 140 Tokens; revisit after the eye-check if a full mat feels wrong.
7. **Build it, then eye-check** (owner's pick over a screenshot page first), in the week-end
   eye-check.

## For the balancer

Reach 240 u, closest spacing 128 u: **12 places** around a node. **Amended by the owner later on
2026-11-26** (balancer's interview): a node gives 15 seconds of work and is back in a minute, so a
gatherer needs **4 or 5 nodes** to stay busy, and a **gathering site has about 5 places for
buildings**; **a maker's station about 8**. (The first answer, about 4 places on nodes and 8 for
buildings, is replaced.) BALANCE_RULES sections 2, 5 and 8 hold the numbers.

# Crime: steal tables and the Hideout

*Owner interview 2026-11-25 (planning agent, standing in for the content agent). The mechanic is
[concept_skill_identities.md](concept_skill_identities.md) "Crime" and brief 810 K4; coins in steal
tables are [concept_gold_coins.md](concept_gold_coins.md) point 13. The content agent turns this into
the CMS pack (T-663). Amounts and odds are the ledger's.*

## Rulings (owner 2026-11-25)

1. **A steal table holds what the enemy keeps**: its stores and hoard, never its body parts. Fighting
   gives hides, bones and meat; stealing gives the camp's goods.
2. **A camp's Crime level is its enemy's level** by default, and the content agent may override it.
3. **The Hideout climbs by variants**, like every skill building: higher tiers picked from a menu.
4. **Every regular camp in the game gets a steal table** in the first pack. Bosses are never robbed.

## The Hideout's variants

Higher tiers at Crime 20, 40, 60, 80, each picked from: **Speed** (faster thefts), **Lucky** (the
table's rare lines more often), **XP**, and **Quiet** (lower caught odds). ⚠️ *Quiet is a new effect*:
the rule exists (caught odds, brief 810 K4) but no effect lowers it yet; a small code ticket with K4.

# Content roadmap: Crafting

*Part of session 4 of [concept_content_roadmap.md](concept_content_roadmap.md). Planning agent's list from the owner's answers, 2026-11-04. Names and rough levels are decided here; numbers are the ledger's.*

## The owner's rulings (2026-11-04)

- **Ranged armour**: "Same Hat/Chest/Gloves/Boots/Shield Leather tiers and Dragonscale Armor."
- **Gems are cut, with no tool, then set** into Smithing's bands.
- **Parchment and Books, Planks, Fishing Rods, Traps and Bait, Charcoal and Torches** stay or join.
- **Not here**: Bricks, Glass, Rope, Bottles, Containers, ceramics.
- **Stealth and Magic armour and String are Tailor's.** Crafting makes items, not Tokens (2026-10-24).

## The shape

- **Five armour tiers, five pieces each**: Leather, Hard, Studded, Drake, Dragonscale.
- **Nails get their job**: Copper in rods and traps, Iron in Hard Leather, Mythril in Studded Leather.
- **Planks for five woods**: Oak, Fir, Maple, Mahogany and Ebony, the ones Construction asks for. Birch and Cedar go to Enchanting as raw wood (review, 2026-11-05; amends "Planks for all seven woods").
- **Six gems cut, 24 pieces of jewellery** for your drawn silver and gold.
- **76 recipes, 23 levels open.**

**Art: 46 have, 27 adapt, 3 new.** You have all 24 pieces of jewellery, six cut and six uncut gems, seven planks (Birch and Cedar go unused), three scales, and leather gloves and boots. To draw: a Leather Shield, Parchment and a Blank Book; most armour is recolours.

## Planning agent's calls

- **Names are suggestions**, except Leather and Dragonscale.
- **Tiers 4 and 5 use your blue and red scales**, from drakes and dragons.
- **Enchanting's Tomes now use a Blank Book**, not Parchment alone.

## Moved or changed from today

- **Leather Hat 15 becomes 6, Leather Chest 21 becomes 10; Leather 20 becomes 5**, and comes from Hide, not Lizard Scale.
- **Fishing Rod** uses String in place of Beeswax Comb, and Leather no longer uses Lizard Scale; both become weird items in [Alchemy](content_alchemy.md).
- **"Search a Bird Nest" retires** with the Bank's Use button (brief 400).

## Open links

- **Planks** must be asked for by Construction's buildings (wood and buildings).
- **Mining**: uncut gems. **Enemies**: Hide, Blue and Red Scales.
- **A Leather Shield** for Ranged: Shields lend defence; how it differs from metal ones is the ledger's.

## Jewellery effects (owner ruling 2026-11-21; effects the content agent's pick)

Rings boost work, amulets boost combat, each with an effect of its own; gold doubles silver.

| Gem | Ring (work) | Amulet (combat) |
|---|---|---|
| Ruby | Quick Hands: work 5% faster | Valour: +1 damage |
| Sapphire | Scholar: +5% XP | Frostward: +10 Elemental resistance |
| Emerald | Green Touch: what grows nearby grows 10% faster | Second Wind: heals 10 below half health |
| Topaz | Prospector: 2% chance of an Uncut Topaz from any work | Keen Eye: +5 accuracy |
| Amethyst | Thrift: 5% chance nothing is used up | Hearty: food heals 20% more |
| Diamond | Fortune: 5% chance an item comes out doubled | Brilliance: +5 crit chance |

# Content roadmap: Crafting

*Part of session 4 of [concept_content_roadmap.md](concept_content_roadmap.md). Planning agent's list from the owner's answers, 2026-11-04. Names and rough levels are decided here; numbers are the ledger's.*

## The owner's rulings (2026-11-04)

- **Ranged armour**: "Same Hat/Chest/Gloves/Boots/Shield Leather tiers and Dragonscale Armor."
- **Gems are cut, with no tool, then set** into Smithing's bands.
- **Parchment and Books, Planks, Fishing Rods, Traps and Bait, Charcoal and Torches** stay or join.
- **Not here**: Bricks, Glass, Rope, Bottles, Containers, ceramics.
- **Stealth and Magic armour and String are Tailor's.** Crafting makes items, not Tokens (2026-10-24).

## The shape

- **Five armour tiers, five pieces each**: Leather, Hard, Studded, Drake, Dragonscale.
- **Nails get their job**: Copper in rods and traps, Iron in Hard Leather, Mythril in Studded Leather.
- **Planks for all seven woods**, at the ladder's levels.
- **Six gems cut, 24 pieces of jewellery** for your drawn silver and gold.
- **76 recipes, 23 levels open.**

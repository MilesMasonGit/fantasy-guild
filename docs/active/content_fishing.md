# Content roadmap: Fishing

*Part of session 2 of [concept_content_roadmap.md](concept_content_roadmap.md). Planning agent's list from the owner's answers, 2026-11-04. Names and rough levels are decided here; weights and timings are the ledger's.*

## The owner's rulings (2026-11-04)

- **Better tools gate fish.** ⚠️ Amends "a tool's tier never gates" ([GDD](../reference/GDD.md), brief 230 S5; `concept_skill_loops.md`).
- **Three tools: Rod, Trap, Harpoon.**
- **Special waters: Shipwreck, Oyster Bed.**
- **Other catch: Seaweed.**
- **Kept**: water never runs out; water type, bait and tool choose the catch; bait is optional; better bait shifts the odds.

## Planning agent's calls

- **Waters stay Coast, River, Lake.** The Shrimp Coast goes, as ruled in October. Bog Pool could join River and Lake with the same fish; it isn't needed.
- **Bait stays Shrimp Bait and Worm Bait**: no more bait kinds until something needs it.
- **Harpoons on Adamantine and Darkmetal**, because those are Smithing's open levels. ⚠️ The first Harpoon asks for Smithing 74 for a fish at Fishing 60, so Fishing can wait on Smithing a long while. An earlier metal would mean giving up a Smithing item, or a level bringing two recipes.

## What this changes

- **Cooking's fish levels** follow when its list is realigned to the 20-level tiers.
- **Today's Coast** gives Tropical Fish, Salmon and Swordfish with a plain rod, and Rivers give Salmon: those pools change.
- **Tool gating** needs a rule: a water's catch pool asks for a tool of a certain tier, which it doesn't today (to check when briefed).
- **Seaweed and Pearls** need their users, or they go by the no-clutter rule.
- **A Fishing Hut's Wider Reach and Rare Finds** variants follow ([Construction](content_construction.md)).

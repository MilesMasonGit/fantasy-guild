# D4: Screens and drag-and-drop, read deep

*Reviewer session D4, 2026-11-17, read at commit **18d91bdc** in the worktree `.claude/worktrees/rw`.
The fourth session of the deep read the owner asked for on 2026-11-15: hunt bugs a player can hit,
one area properly. Method: the area was cut into eight pieces (the drag-and-drop layer; the mat
screen; the Token card and recipes; the hero dock and drawers; the Bank and Shop; the Guild Hall;
the world map; notices, Limes, the Encyclopedia and the modals) and two cross-cutting traces (does

where two reports disagreed the disagreement is written into the finding. Every finding was then

minimised desktop window's timers are throttled enough to trigger a quiet catch-up (CR4-174); and
whether Alt-Tab with the button held leaves the Guild Hall's sky following the pointer (CR4-192).
Nothing was run: no tests, no bench, no game. Focus: what a player is told wrongly, cannot do, or
loses.*

## Summary for the owner

**What this area does for the player.** Everything the player sees and touches: the mat, Token
cards and recipe lists, the hero bar and drawers, the Bank and Shop, the Guild Hall, the world map,
banners, Limes, the Encyclopedia and the settings windows, and the drag-and-drop that joins them.

**How healthy it is.** Sound where it counts. No screen loses or duplicates an item, a Token or a
payment: the Shop's buy and refund, the Bank's slot counting, settling and travel all match what
the engine does, and double presses there are harmless. The world map's buttons agree with the
engine's refusals, and its prices match what is paid. A drag that is cancelled, or whose source
vanishes mid-drag, cleans up after itself. The faults are of three kinds: numbers that stand still
when they should move, drops that land on something hidden underneath, and windows that cover or
close each other. No finding is at P1.

**The three that matter most.**

1. **A fighting hero's health bar stands still** (CR4-168). On the hero bar and in the Hero
   drawer, a hero's health does not move while they are being hit, then jumps at the kill or the
   knock-out. The one number a player watches in a fight is the one that does not update.
2. **"While you were away" can be stuck under a window** (CR4-170). If Settings, Edit Hero or Text
   size was open when the catch-up starts (after a sleep, say), the summary shows but cannot be
   clicked, and clicks go to the hidden window behind it. Settled from the library's source; one
   try in the desktop build proves it.
3. **The "no loadout changes mid-fight" ruling is not built yet** (CR4-172). Seven ways of
   changing a hero's slots (dragging onto a slot, right-click, dropping on the hero figure,
   dragging to the Bank, hand-overs, which touch the giver too, and more) reach the engine with no
   fight check in the screen or the engine. This is the work list for the owner's 2026-11-15
   ruling, not a new defect.

**Also worth knowing.** After the PC sleeps, a Token's "!" for a stalled camp, Stump or Field can
be missing or stay up after the stall is over (CR4-169). Travel wipes every hero's level-up alerts
(CR4-173). A knocked-out hero lights every Token green as "ready" though every order is refused
(CR4-178). A flag's hover says "Working: Furnace" in green while the Furnace waits for Coal
(CR4-179). The world map opens by itself under an open Bank or Shop when the last lesson is done
(CR4-175). A double-click on Upgrade in the Guild Hall buys the next rank too (CR4-195, the
owner's call).

**Counts.** 30 findings, all verified by a second reader, none refuted (18 confirmed as written,
12 with corrections folded in): 4 at P2, 26 at P3. Twenty-five are bugs a player can meet today,
one is the work list for a ruling already made, three are the owner's decisions and two wait for
content or a crash.

**For the director.** Three steps need one try in the desktop build, not a bench: the summary under
an open Settings window (CR4-170), whether a minimised window drops the selection once a minute
(CR4-174), and the Guild Hall sky after an Alt-Tab (CR4-192). CR4-172 should be built with the
engine-side guard as well as the screen's, so a route added later cannot bypass it.

### CR4-168  A fighting hero's health on the hero bar and in the Hero drawer stands still until the kill or the knock-out
- **Tag**: bug
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P2 / S / low
- **Confidence**: verified (both readers traced every publisher of the events the two bars listen to)
- **Where**: `src/ui/components/dock/DockHeroFigure.jsx:128-146`, `src/ui/components/drawer/HeroInspectionSheet.jsx:59-74`, `src/ui/components/drawer/HeroInspectionSheet.jsx:119`, `src/ui/components/board/MatHero.jsx:38-48`, `src/ui/hooks/useGameState.js:138-142`, `src/systems/combat/CombatAttackProcessor.js:82-87`, `src/systems/combat/CombatAttackProcessor.js:161-166`, `src/systems/hero/logic/HeroState.js:52-59`, `src/systems/hero/RegenSystem.js:40-63`, `src/systems/combat/CombatProcessor.js:71`, `src/systems/combat/CombatResolutionProcessor.js:45`, `src/systems/combat/BoardCombat.js:540`, `src/systems/effects/LiveEffects.js:218`, `src/systems/effects/LiveEffects.js:291`, `src/systems/effects/LiveEffects.js:457`, `src/systems/effects/LiveEffects.js:307-327`, `src/systems/effects/LiveEffects.js:436-441`, `src/systems/board/DealDamage.js:85`, `src/config/FormulaRegistry.js:186`, `src/ui/components/board/MatBoard.jsx:247`, `src/ui/components/board/MatBoard.jsx:256-263`
- **What**: While a hero trades blows, the bar over the hero's head on the mat and the fight window drop with every hit, but the hero's bar on the hero bar at the foot of the screen, and the large HP bar in their Hero drawer, keep the value they had. They jump to the truth only when something else about any hero is announced: the kill (its XP) or the knock-out. To see it: new game, one hero, send the hero at the first enemy and watch both bars: the mat's falls hit by hit, the hero bar's stays full. Food eaten in the fight is missed the same way, and so are a poison's ticks: the bar is refreshed when the poison lands and when it ends, never by the ticks in between. With several heroes at work the bar moves in irregular jumps (whenever another hero finishes a work cycle, regenerates or eats), often a few seconds behind, which hides the fault without fixing it; with one hero, or every hero fighting, it is wrong for the whole fight. The hero bar is on every screen and is the only health the player can see on the World Map, in the Guild Hall and under the open Bank, so a hero can go from "full" straight to knocked out.
- **Evidence**: An enemy's hit calls `HeroManager.modifyHeroHp` (`CombatAttackProcessor.js:161`), which writes `hp.current` in place and publishes nothing (`HeroState.js:52-59`); the swing then publishes `COMBAT_ENEMY_ATTACK` only (`:163-166`). Eating in a fight publishes `COMBAT_HERO_ATE` (`:85`), `HERO_CONSUMED` and the Bank's `INVENTORY_UPDATED`; a status tick publishes `STATUS_DOT_TICK` (`LiveEffects.js:438`); a rule's damage (`DealDamage.js:85`) publishes nothing unless it downs the hero. The mat knows this: `MatHero.jsx:38-48` says the engine changes `hp` in place "and says so only through these", and listens to `HEROES_UPDATED`, `COMBAT_ENEMY_ATTACK`, `COMBAT_HERO_ATE`, `STATUS_DOT_TICK`, `GAME_RESET`. The hero bar's figure listens to `HEROES_UPDATED`, `HERO_EQUIPMENT_CHANGED`, `GAME_RESET` only (`DockHeroFigure.jsx:142`); the drawer's sheet to `HEROES_UPDATED`, `HERO_LEVELED`, `GAME_RESET` only (`HeroInspectionSheet.jsx:71`); `useGameState` reads the store only when a named event fires (`useGameState.js:138-142`). What could have saved it and does not: every publisher of `HEROES_UPDATED` was listed; in a fight the only ones are a live effect being applied, expiring or removed (`LiveEffects.js:218`, `:244`, `:291`, `:341`, `:381`, `:407`, `:457`; enemies or elixirs with effects only; a damage-over-time tick itself does not notify), the kill's XP (`SkillSystem.addXP`, once per kill, `CombatResolutionProcessor.js:45`) and the defeat (`BoardCombat.js:540`). Regeneration skips a hero whose status is `combat` (`RegenSystem.js:42`, set at `CombatProcessor.js:71`) and publishes only when some other hero regenerated or ate: regeneration runs every 10 s (`REGEN_CONFIG.hp.interval`, `FormulaRegistry.js:186`), but a non-fighting hero eating sets `regenOccurred` on any tick (`RegenSystem.js:56-58`), so that refresh can come sooner. `endSwingEffects` and `endAttackEffects` do not notify (`LiveEffects.js:307-327`). A smaller cousin (the stale-screens reader): the mat's own "hurt" flag that keeps a working hero's bar up (`MatBoard.jsx:247`, events `:256-263`) misses a rule's damage to a working hero until the next regen step, up to 10 s. **Found independently by two readers** (the hero dock; the stale-screens trace), both verified, both P2.
- **Fix sketch**: Give the hero bar's figure and the drawer's sheet the same HP event list the mat's bar has (`COMBAT_ENEMY_ATTACK`, `COMBAT_HERO_ATE`, `STATUS_DOT_TICK`), filtered to their hero and shared from one place; or have `modifyHeroHp`'s callers announce one hero-health event. Must not change: the projections stay flat (the bar redraws only when the number moves, the selector contract), the roster's separate status store, and no engine-visible work or random draw is added.
- **Verified by**: Opus verifier A (read-only, given the finding's text only), 2026-11-17: confirmed with corrections (folded in); re-traced every `HEROES_UPDATED` publisher reachable mid-fight and the three UI subscriptions, found no rescue; corrected the "every 10 s at most" (eating by a non-fighting hero publishes on any tick) and noted that a status landing or expiring does refresh the bar.
- **State**: ticket T-461 (triaged 2026-11-17)

### CR4-169  After the PC sleeps, or any catch-up in mid-play, a Token's "!" for a stalled camp, Stump or Field can be missing, or stay up after the stall is over
- **Tag**: bug
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P2 / S / low
- **Confidence**: verified
- **Where**: `src/ui/components/board/TokenBubbles.jsx:171-189`, `src/ui/components/board/TokenBubbles.jsx:193`, `src/ui/components/board/tokenEvents.js:38-44`, `src/systems/board/SpawnerSystem.js:549-570`, `src/systems/board/Growth.js:133-144`, `src/systems/board/Growth.js:165-169`, `src/systems/board/Growth.js:229-230`, `src/systems/board/Fields.js:204-208`, `src/systems/core/CatchUp.js:272-285`, `src/systems/core/CatchUp.js:402-408`, `src/ui/components/board/MatBoard.jsx:605`, `src/ui/components/board/MatCapBadge.jsx:88-92`, `src/ui/components/board/flagText.js:180`
- **What**: The problem mark on a Token nobody works (a camp waiting because the mat is at its Token cap or has no room, a Stump with no Forest in reach or no seedling, a Field with no seed) is kept by the Token's own drawing from the alert's change events. A catch-up plays with those events unheard, and the engine announces an alert only when it changes, so whatever changed during the catch-up is never shown. Leave the game running with a Wheat Field sown and few Wheat Seeds and let the PC sleep for an hour: on return the Field has stopped for want of seed and wears no "!", and resting the pointer on it shows no problem on its idle plate. The reverse too: a Field or camp stalled before the sleep and going again during it keeps its "!" though it is working. It stays wrong until that Token's alert next changes, or the Token is drawn afresh (travel away and back, a reload). Meanwhile the Token count popover, the Upkeep summary and the hero bar's hover line read the alerts live, so they disagree with the mat. A fresh load is not affected: the Tokens are first drawn after the load's catch-up.
- **Evidence**: `stuck` and `growth` are React state, read from the engine only at mount and when `instanceId` changes (`TokenBubbles.jsx:172`, `:175`, `:182`, `:185`), then set from the payloads of `SPAWNER_ALERT_CHANGED` and `GROWTH_ALERT_CHANGED` (`:176-178`, `:186-188`), which reach it through `subscribeBy`, a `UI_LISTENER` (`tokenEvents.js:38-44`) the quiet bus skips (`CatchUp.js:272-285`). `SpawnerSystem.syncAlerts` and `Growth.setGrowthAlert` publish only on a change against their own kept map (`SpawnerSystem.js:559-563`, `Growth.js:134-143`), so after the catch-up there is nothing to re-announce, and the catch-up's end publishes only `GAME_RESET`, `HEROES_UPDATED` and `INVENTORY_UPDATED` (`CatchUp.js:402-408`). Nothing in `TokenBubbles` listens to `GAME_RESET`. Checked for a rescue: `MatToken` is keyed by the Token's id (`MatBoard.jsx:605`) and a surviving Token keeps its id through a catch-up, so it is not remounted; `useTokenDetail` does re-read on `GAME_RESET`, but its projection carries `instance.alert` (a worked Token's alert), not these two. **How it relates**: the mat-screen reader raised the same component as a lead for an in-session load (`TokenBubbles.jsx:172-189` with `Growth.js:165-169`, `:229-230`); the stale-screens reader found that no in-session load exists (the slot screen has no way back, and "Load as I left it" reloads the page, `useUIModals.js:192-195`), which settles that lead, and this catch-up path is the live form of it.
- **Fix sketch**: Re-read `spawnerAlertOf` and `growthAlertOf` on `GAME_RESET`, or carry both in `tokenDetailOf`, which already wakes on it. Must not change: no polling, and the per-Token routing (one bus subscription per event type).
- **Verified by**: Opus verifier A (read-only, given the finding's text only), 2026-11-17: confirmed; traced the quiet bus during a catch-up, the change-only alert publishers and the catch-up's end events, and found nothing that re-reads `TokenBubbles`' alert state or remounts it; `MatCapBadge` does hear `GAME_RESET`, which is why the count popover disagrees with the mat.
- **State**: ticket T-462 (triaged 2026-11-17)

### CR4-170  The "While you were away" summary cannot be clicked if Settings, Edit Hero or Text size was open when the PC went to sleep; clicks go through it to the window hidden underneath
- **Tag**: bug
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P2 / M / medium
- **Confidence**: reasoned (settled from the Headless UI 2.2.9 source by the second reader: `#react-root`, and with it the summary, gets `inert = true` while Settings, Edit Hero or Text size is open, and the summary paints above the window, since nothing makes a stacking context on `#react-root`. Still open: that WebView2 then lets the clicks fall through to Settings, which is Chromium's handling of `inert` (hit-testing as if absent) and the expected result. One try in a desktop build settles it: open Settings, sleep the PC for three minutes, wake, click the summary's buttons and see whether a Settings control under them reacts)
- **Where**: `src/ui/ReactRoot.jsx:443-453`, `src/ui/components/hud/CatchUpOverlay.jsx:65-73`, `src/ui/components/base/GIModal.jsx:37`, `node_modules/@headlessui/react/dist/components/dialog/dialog.js` (the `useInertOthers` call, `disallowed`), `node_modules/@headlessui/react/dist/hooks/use-inert-others.js`, `index.html:25`, `src/main.jsx:106`, `src/tailwind.css:207-209`, `src/ui/utils/escapeLayers.js:19-27`, `src/systems/core/GameLoop.js:66-68`, `src/systems/core/CatchUp.js:99-113`, `src/systems/core/CatchUp.js:329`, `src/config/loopConstants.js:43`, `src/ui/modals/SettingsModal.jsx:163`
- **What**: Open Settings (or a hero's Edit window, or Text size), leave the game running, let the PC sleep for two minutes or more, wake it. The catch-up bar plays and the summary appears on top of the Settings window, but neither of its buttons reacts to the mouse. Each click lands instead on whatever Settings control lies under that point, unseen behind the summary: a toggle, "Save Game Now", or "Reset to defaults", which resets every option at once with no question asked. Esc only asks "Return to the Guild?" and a second Esc cancels the question; it never confirms, and Enter does nothing for the summary. The player sees a summary they cannot close. The ways out are blind: click the spot where Settings' Done or X sits under the summary, or press Enter while the keyboard focus happens to be on Settings' X, or close the game (the desktop close saves, and accepts the catch-up, CR4-164). The game keeps running underneath; nothing is lost.
- **Evidence**: Settings, Edit Hero and Text size are all `GIModal`, a Headless UI 2.2.9 `Dialog` (`GIModal.jsx:37`). While a Dialog is open it runs `useInertOthers(open, { disallowed: () => [mainTreeNode.closest("body > *:not(#headlessui-portal-root)")] })`, and `use-inert-others.js` sets `aria-hidden="true"` and `inert = true` on that element, which here is the game's `#react-root` (`index.html:25`, `main.jsx:106`). The Dialog itself is drawn in `#headlessui-portal-root`. `CatchUpOverlay` is not portalled: it is the last child of `ReactRoot` (`ReactRoot.jsx:453`), inside the inert root, as a `fixed inset-0` box at `--z-catch-up` 350, above the modal's 300 (`CatchUpOverlay.jsx:70-72`, `tailwind.css:207-209`); nothing between `#react-root` and the overlay makes a stacking context, so it paints over Settings while being untargetable. Its `autoFocus` button cannot take focus inside an inert tree, and the Dialog's focus trap holds focus in the window. Esc reaches the summary because its escape layer was pushed after the window's (`escapeLayers.js:23`), and its handler is `setAsking(a => (a ? null : 'return'))` (`CatchUpOverlay.jsx:65`), which toggles the question and nothing else. The trigger is real: a modal does not pause the loop, the live loop's missed time goes to `CatchUp` (`GameLoop.js:66-68`, `CatchUp.js:99-113`), and a gap of `SHOW_GAP_MS` = 2 minutes shows the bar and the summary (`loopConstants.js:43`, `CatchUp.js:329`). "Reset to defaults" has no confirm step (`SettingsModal.jsx:163`). What could have saved it and does not: the overlay has no portal; nothing closes an open modal on `CATCH_UP_STARTED`. A catch-up on load is not affected: no `GIModal` is open then, and the slot screen is not a Dialog. **Found independently by two readers** (notices and modals; the actions trace), both reasoned on the same step, both P2.
- **Fix sketch**: Close any open window (Settings, Edit Hero, Text size) when a shown catch-up starts, or draw `CatchUpOverlay` through a portal outside `#react-root` (then check its focus against the Dialog's tab lock). Must not change: a window still never closes on a click outside it; the summary's two confirm steps; Esc cancelling a question; the layer order for a catch-up on load.
- **Verified by**: Opus verifier A (read-only, given the finding's text only), 2026-11-17: confirmed; read Headless UI's `dialog.js`, `use-inert-others.js` and `use-root-containers.js`, settled that `#react-root` is made inert with the overlay inside it and that the overlay paints above the window, and confirmed the Escape path (the capture listener stops Headless UI's own Escape); the click going through stays for one desktop try.
- **State**: held: owner desktop try pending (2026-11-17)

### CR4-171  An item flicked quickly from a long Bank list onto a hero on the hero bar can re-order the Bank instead of equipping, with the equip sound
- **Tag**: bug
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / M / medium
- **Confidence**: verified (the second reader read dnd-kit's auto-scroll in `node_modules` and settled the open step: the list scrolls itself to its end within a fraction of a second, so only a quick drop meets the fault; the tile and hero areas were worked out by hand, not measured in a browser)
- **Where**: `src/ui/dnd/DndKit.jsx:63-66`, `src/ui/dnd/DndKit.jsx:96-108`, `src/ui/dnd/DndKit.jsx:115-134`, `src/ui/dnd/DndKit.jsx:483-499`, `src/ui/components/drawer/BankTab.jsx:103-118`, `src/ui/components/drawer/BankTab.jsx:251-258`, `src/ui/components/drawer/BankTab.jsx:269`, `src/ui/components/drawer/BankTab.jsx:329-334`, `src/ui/components/drawer/BankDrawer.jsx:73`, `src/ui/components/dock/DockHeroFigure.jsx:71-88`, `src/ui/components/dock/dockHeroView.js:15`, `src/ui/components/dock/dockHeroView.js:25`, `src/ui/components/dock/DockEquipmentGrid.jsx:121-131`, `src/styles/main.css:287`, `src/ui/dnd/DndKit.jsx:233-237`, `node_modules/@dnd-kit/core/dist/core.esm.js:1994-2037`, `node_modules/@dnd-kit/core/dist/core.esm.js:831-885`
- **What**: With the Bank open and the open tab holding more rows than fit (the list scrolls), drag an item tile down onto a hero on the hero bar, the Bank's main "equip" gesture. Rows scrolled out of sight below the list still have their drop boxes, and those boxes lie exactly over the hero bar's 64 px strip. While a hidden tile under the pointer is smaller in area than the hero's box, the hidden tile takes the drop: the item is not equipped, it is moved in the Bank to just before that hidden tile (so it vanishes from where it was), and the equip sound plays. From a hero's loadout slot dropped on another bar hero (a hand-over), the hidden tile instead unequips it to the Bank. While the search box is in use the hidden tile refuses: the drop is a miss, the ghost springs back and the `invalid` sound plays. What keeps it rare: dnd-kit's auto-scroll scrolls the Bank list on its own, at about 2,000 px/s or faster, from the moment the pointer enters the list's bottom 18 % on the way down, and stops only at the list's end; once there, no tile box lies below the list's visible edge and the hero takes the drop. So a held, deliberate drop equips correctly, and only a quick flick-and-release (or a release just as the pointer arrives over the bar) lands in the fraction of a second before the list has scrolled away. Repro: a window about 1440 px wide (tile width works out at about 98 px), 50 or more item kinds in tab 1 (Bank tabs hold 64), do not scroll, flick the first item onto the middle hero on the bar and release at once. At the default maximised 1920 px the tile is about 105 px and the hero wins, and 64 kinds do not overflow there, so a full-screen 1080p player does not see it; smaller or windowed play (the window can be as small as 1024 x 700) can.
- **Evidence**: `pointerWithinBoxes` collects every enabled target whose measured box holds the pointer; `smallestWithin` sorts by `rank` (the tile and `dock-hero-drop-*` are both `DND_SURFACE.DRAWER`, rank 0), then by `rect.width * rect.height` (`DndKit.jsx:96-108`, `:115-134`). A tile is square, between 96 px (6rem; html is 16 px, `main.css:287`) and about 108 px wide (`BankTab.jsx:269`), so 9,216 to about 11,700 px²; a bar hero's box is slot width (160, less when crowded) x `DOCK_STRIP_PX` 64 = 10,240 px² at most (`dockHeroView.js:15`, `:25`). So for tile widths under about 101 px (roughly half of all window widths, since the width cycles between 96 and 108 as columns are added) the tile sorts first and becomes dnd-kit's `over`. `handleDragEnd` asks only `over` (`DndKit.jsx:483-499`): the tile's `accepts` is true for a Bank item when not searching (`canReorder && p.itemId !== entry.id`) and for anything from a hero slot, and `handleTileDrop` (`BankTab.jsx:103-118`) then re-orders (`InventoryManager.setGroupOrder`) or unequips (`unequipOrAnnounce`). It returns `undefined`, which `handleDragEnd` counts as success (`!== false`), hence the `item_equip` clip. The code already knows this class of fault: `DockEquipmentGrid.jsx:121-131` (`pointerOutsideList`) says dnd-kit's boxes know nothing of clipping and that a small hidden target "wins the drop", and guards the loadout slots; Bank tiles have no such guard. Checked for a save: droppables are measured at drag start for all containers, scrolled-out ones included (`core.esm.js:1994-2037`); nothing filters by visibility; `dropBox` only shifts boxes by scrolling done during the drag (`DndKit.jsx:72-93`). The partial save is dnd-kit's auto-scroll: `AUTO_SCROLL` is on with a vertical threshold of 0.18 (`DndKit.jsx:233-237`); while the hidden tile is `over`, the Bank list (`overflow-y-auto`, `BankTab.jsx:258`) is the ancestor scrolled (`core.esm.js:2956`); the scroll-down test `bottom >= scrollContainerRect.bottom - threshold.height` has no upper limit (`:870`), so a pointer over the strip below the list still counts as near its bottom; its speed is `10 * (distance / threshold)` per 5 ms tick (`:873`, `:1772`); it stops at `isBottom` (`:815`), and collisions are recomputed on scroll (`:2978`). The search case's miss plays the `invalid` sound (`DndKit.jsx:510-512`). The list ends exactly where the hero bar's strip begins (`BankDrawer.jsx:73`). **How it relates**: CR4-181 is the same collision rule with the Hero drawer covering the Bank; the hero-dock reader's lead on a hero's head standing above the strip at a large fit (`dockHeroView.js:53-58`) is a third way into it.
- **Fix sketch**: Give Bank tiles the guard the loadout slots have (a tile is not a collision at all when the pointer is outside its scroll list's visible box), ideally once in `pointerWithinBoxes` for every target inside a scrolling box, which would also serve CR4-181. Must not change: a visible tile still beats the list behind it, and tabs and slots keep beating tiles.

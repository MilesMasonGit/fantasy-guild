## Session summary (for the owner)

**What this area is.** Everything the player looks at: the mat with its Tokens, heroes, flags,
rings, bars and plates; the one-shot effects (numbers rising, sparks, knockouts, speech); the
ground picture and the ambient life on and over it; the hero bar, the Bank and Shop drawers, the
notification stubs and banners, the fight windows, the Atlas and the Encyclopedia; and the
stylesheets that colour and animate them.

**How healthy it is.** Good, and markedly better than round 3 left it: the shared frame clocks
(one step clock for the bars, one due clock for every sprite), the walkers moved by a transform
written straight to the element, the stack that keeps each Token's place, the terrain built off
the frame and kept in the mat's own layer, the loot bob on the clock: round 3's biggest costs are
fixed and the draw bench shows it (the realistic board at 1× does its frame work in 0.51 ms
median). What this round found is one real mechanism behind an open ticket, and a layer of
per-pointer-move and per-render work that the project's own rules forbid.

**The three findings that matter most.**
1. **CR4-046**: the cause of T-333 (a hidden page piles up elements and listeners). Every one-shot
   effect on the mat (a damage number, a spark, the ring blink on a finished cycle, a knockout)
   adds an element and removes it only when its animation finishes. A hidden page draws no frame,
   so nothing ever finishes, and the engine keeps firing them: 294 elements and 136 listeners a
   minute in the soak, all freed in one burst when the window comes back. The camera code already
   shows the fix (a timer that lands it anyway).
2. **CR4-048**: on every pointer move across the mat, the game queries the whole document for
   hit-test boxes and measures each, measures the mat, and scans every Token. The project's own
   performance rules forbid layout reads per pointer move; this is where drag smoothness (T-201)
   is most likely lost.
3. **CR4-050**: fifteen pollers tick on timers whether or not anything changed and whether or not
   the window is visible, and one of them recomputes the whole upkeep summary over every Token on
   every Bank change. Each poll is a React commit for nothing.

**Counts.** 15 findings: 1 `memory` (measured), 10 `perf` (reasoned, P2 to P3), 1 `bug`, 2 `cleanup`,
1 `docs`; 0 `owner-decision`. Verified: see each finding's `Verified by`.

**For the director to measure first.** (1) The soak's hidden phase (`npm run bench:soak`, 5 min
hidden) after CR4-046's gate: the node and listener slopes should fall to about zero. (2) A
pointer-move trace on the 128 board (`bench:profile`) for `updateAlphaPointerEvents` and
`MatBoard`'s `handlePointerMove`, and `bench:drag` for T-201, before and after CR4-048. (3)
`bench:draw --switches` on a board with several stations awaiting a recipe choice, for CR4-051. (4) For the ground: in a perf build on a coast Region, the `fg-perf:ground-plan` and `ground-screen` measures (DevTools, Timings) and the sum of the ambient `putImageData` box areas per second at idle, which separates build cost (CR4-057, 058) from steady-state paint (CR4-059).

**What was read and found healthy (so nobody re-checks it).** `frameClock.js`: three clocks, each
running only while subscribed, all stopped by `setDrawingPaused`, which `drawingPause.js` drives
from `visibilitychange` and the desktop app's minimise event. `AnimatedHeroSprite` and the enemy
sprite step on the due clock and write the frame to the element; `workBar` writes its fill on the
step clock only when the filled pixel count changes; walkers glide by a transform written on
`HEROES_WALKED`/`ENEMIES_WALKED` without a React render; `MatBoard` memoises every surface and
keys its stack order by value; `TerrainCanvas` builds the plan off the frame, settles the fit for
250 ms and keeps a CPU-backed canvas; `ParticleOverlay`'s loop sleeps when empty; the
notification column has no backdrop blur and removes stubs and banners on timers; every
`EventBus.subscribe` the readers checked returns its unsubscribe from the effect.

## Findings

### CR4-046  One-shot mat effects remove themselves only when their animation finishes, so a hidden page piles them up (T-333's mechanism)
- **Tag**: memory
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P2 / S / low
- **Confidence**: measured (the symptom: `baseline/soak.txt`, 2026-11-07, 44b75a7a, cap128, headless Chrome 156) and verified (the mechanism, read from the code)
- **Where**: the two helpers every effect uses: `src/ui/components/board/fx/matFx.js:47-61` (`playAt`: `host.appendChild(el)`, `el.animate(...)`, `anim.onfinish = anim.oncancel = () => el.remove()`) and `src/ui/components/board/fx/tokenFx.js:19-32` (`playIn`, the same); what calls them on engine events, with no check of the pause: `matFx.js:390-414` (`watchMatFx`: `EFFECT_FIRED` → `spark` and `floatNumber`; `COMBAT_HERO_ATE` → `heroFlash`; `HERO_CONSUMED` → `usedBubble`, capped at 3 per hero; `COMBAT_RESOLVED` → `knockoutGhost`, a box with three copies and four puffs), `src/ui/components/board/fx/fightFloats.js:111-131,139-141` (`playFloat` on every hit, miss, crit, heal and status tick of every fight, uncapped), `src/ui/components/board/TokenHitArt.jsx:118-121` (`ringBlink` on every `CYCLE_COMPLETE` of every worked Token), `:122-128` (`echoRing`, four spans, on every `EFFECT_FIRED`), `:94-115` (the hit's tint and two afterimages, made inside `later(...)` on the paused due clock, so they queue in `dueSubs` and mount on resume); what gates them: only `isDrawn(...)`, a dev switch; `grep -rn "isDrawingPaused\|document.hidden" src/ui` outside `perfHarness.js` and `drawingPause.js`: nothing; the pattern that handles it: `src/ui/components/board/HallCamera.jsx:17-22,128-137` ("A hidden window draws no frames, so its glide never starts or reports done: a timer lands it")
- **What**: Every number that rises off a Token, every spark, every blink when a cycle finishes, every knockout puff is an element appended to the mat with a Web Animation whose `onfinish` removes it. A hidden or minimised window renders no frames, so no animation ever reaches `finished`, and `setDrawingPaused` stops only the three shared clocks: the engine keeps ticking, the bus keeps delivering `CYCLE_COMPLETE`, `EFFECT_FIRED` and the fight moments to their UI listeners, and each one appends another element with two animation handlers. On the realistic board that is a ring blink per completed cycle (2,100 an hour) plus the fights' floats. When the window comes back, the first frame finishes every pending animation at once and every element is removed: exactly the soak's shape (nodes 1,876 → 3,347 over five hidden minutes, back to 1,875 on the next visible sample). The player sees nothing; the cost is memory and a burst of removals on return, and it is unbounded for the length of time the game sits minimised, which for an idle game is hours. The hit copies are the one path that waits properly (their `later` is on the paused clock), and they then all fire at once on resume.
- **Evidence**: `baseline/soak.txt` lines 32-37: hidden phase, DOM nodes +294 a minute, listeners +136 a minute, both restored on the next visible sample; `matFx.js:58-60` and `tokenFx.js:28-30` (`onfinish`/`oncancel` → `remove`, no timer); the grep above (no caller reads the pause); `HallCamera.jsx:128-131` (`landBy`: `setTimeout(() => arrive(glide), wait)` beside `onfinish`).
- **Fix sketch**: Two small changes, either alone enough: (a) in `playAt` and `playIn`, beside `onfinish`, a `setTimeout(done, duration + delay + spare)` as `HallCamera` does (timers do run hidden, throttled to once a second, then once a minute after five hidden minutes, which still bounds the pile to a minute's worth); (b) gate the watchers (`watchMatFx`, `watchFightFloats`, `TokenHitArt`'s three handlers, `later`) on `isDrawingPaused()` so nothing mounts while the window is hidden (an effect that would have ended before the window returns is never missed). (b) is cheaper and also stops the on-resume burst; (a) is the safety net for anything else that animates. `usedBubble` already caps itself. Must not change: how any effect looks while visible; the catch-up (its bus is quiet, so UI listeners never fire there). **Hypothesis to test**: `npm run bench:soak` hidden slope for nodes and listeners falls from +294/+136 a minute to about 0, and T-333 closes.
- **Verified by**: Sonnet verifier, 2026-11-07: confirmed. Read every cited range; grepped `src/` and `bench/` for `isDrawingPaused|document.hidden|visibilityState` (only the definition, the setter, the perf harness and bench scripts); re-read the soak rows (1,877 → 3,347 nodes hidden, back to 1,875 visible; +133 to +137 listeners a step). Line cite for the soak rows corrected.
- **State**: ticket T-333 (triaged 2026-11-07)

### CR4-047  Loot flights booked while the page is hidden never drain until a frame runs, and all land at once
- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / S / low
- **Confidence**: reasoned
- **Where**: `src/ui/components/base/ParticleOverlay.jsx:310-350` (`spawnCollected` on every `SPRITE_COLLECTED`: pushes to `this.booked`, and for a flight to a stub calls `bookFlight`, which holds the stub's count back), `:436-446` (`_nextSlot`: at most 12 flights per burst, but the burst resets after 250 ms quiet, so a trickle of auto-collects is never refused), `:169-181` (`placeBooked` and `update` run only inside the `requestAnimationFrame` loop; `wake` schedules one frame and nothing else), `:449-452` (`_boardPoint`: `document.querySelector('[data-board-origin]')` and a `getBoundingClientRect` per booked flight)
- **What**: Auto-collect keeps collecting while the window is hidden (the engine runs), and each collection books a flight: the particle system is asked to draw it in the next frame, and the stub it flies to holds its number back until the flight lands. No frame comes, so the bookings queue (memory, bounded only by the collections made while hidden) and the stub counts stay frozen at their pre-hide values. On return, every booking's start time is in the past, so `update` lands them all in one frame: one `landFlight` and one `PARTICLE_LANDED` publish each, and one board measurement per booking in `placeBooked`. The same family as CR4-046, but not DOM: a queue and a frozen number.
- **Evidence**: As cited; `ParticleOverlay.jsx:93`'s comment "A collection reads no box" against `_boardPoint`'s `getBoundingClientRect` per booked flight.
- **Fix sketch**: In `spawnCollected`, when `isDrawingPaused()` (or `document.hidden`), skip the drawing and land at once (`landFlight` with no particle, no `bookFlight` hold), so the stub counts stay live and nothing queues; measure the board once per `placeBooked` batch rather than per booking. Must not change: the flight's look when visible; `PARTICLE_LANDED`'s payload (the Hall dips on it).
- **Verified by**: Sonnet verifier, 2026-11-07: confirmed `spawnCollected` (:310-352, `booked.push` :346), `_nextSlot` (:440-446), `placeBooked`/`update` called only inside the rAF loop (:169-170), `_boardPoint` (:449-452). Not re-read: that `update` lands every stale booking in one frame.
- **State**: ticket T-341 (triaged 2026-11-07)

### CR4-048  Every pointer move over the mat queries the whole document and forces layout
- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / M / low
- **Confidence**: reasoned
- **Where**: `src/ui/utils/alphaHitTest.js:283-300` (`updateAlphaPointerEvents`: `document.querySelectorAll('[data-alpha-test]')` and `getBoundingClientRect()` on every match), wired at `:353-357` to `window` `pointermove` with no throttle; `src/ui/components/board/MatBoard.jsx:306-348` (`handlePointerMove`: `el.getBoundingClientRect()` at `:316`, then `Flags.tokenAtPoint(point)`, a scan of every Token with `centreOf` and `artRadiusOf` each (`src/systems/board/Flags.js:202-208,214-221`), then `flagClothOver`); `src/ui/components/base/Tooltip.jsx:41-45,87` (`place`: `offsetWidth`/`offsetHeight` read right after the previous move's transform write, on every move while a tip is open); the rule: `docs/reference/PERFORMANCE.md:155` ("No layout reads in hot paths: no `getBoundingClientRect`, `offsetWidth` or query-then-measure per frame, per pointer move or per tick")
- **What**: Moving the pointer across the mat runs two hit tests, each with a layout read. The alpha test (so a press on a hero's transparent corner reaches the Token behind) walks the whole document for every element that opted in (every hero on the mat: `MatHero.jsx:269` is the only JSX that sets `data-alpha-test`) and measures each; the mat's own hover measures the mat and asks the engine which Token is under the point, scanning all of them. On a busy board with the pointer moving that is one document query, one rectangle per mat hero plus one for the mat, and a distance check per Token (a few hundred on a full mat) per move, at the browser's pointer rate (60 to 240 a second), and the measurements can force a layout if anything is dirty. The project wrote the rule against this after round 3; these three are the places it is not kept. The drag bench's unsmooth pickup and drop (T-201) is where the cost would show, since a drag is a stream of pointer moves.
- **Evidence**: As cited; `grep -rn "data-alpha-test" src/ui`: set only by `MatHero.jsx:269`; read by `alphaHitTest.js`, `pressRouting.js:11` and `DndKit.jsx:324`.
- **Fix sketch**: One hit-test pass per frame, not per move: coalesce `pointermove` into a single `requestAnimationFrame` (or the step clock while a frame is pending), and in it read the mat's rect once (or keep it from a `ResizeObserver`/scroll listener) and reuse it for both the alpha test and the hover; have the alpha test take the element from `e.target.closest('[data-alpha-test]')` instead of querying the document, and measure only that one. Keep `Tooltip`'s `place` to the coalesced frame too. Must not change: which Token is hovered or pressed (the `tokenAtPoint` rule, `pressRouting`), the drag's feel (an eye-check if it does: pickup and hover). **Hypothesis to test**: `bench:drag` pickup and drop longest frames (T-201's numbers) and a pointer-move trace's `updateAlphaPointerEvents` self time, before and after.
- **Verified by**: Sonnet verifier, 2026-11-07: partly; corrected. `tokenAtPoint` is at `Flags.js:214-221` (not :177-187), and only mat heroes carry `data-alpha-test` (no dock figure does), so the per-move cost is smaller than first written; the three unthrottled layout reads per move stand.
- **State**: ticket T-349 (triaged 2026-11-07)

### CR4-050  Fifteen pollers tick whether or not anything changed, none pauses while hidden, and one recomputes the whole upkeep summary on every Bank change
- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / M / low
- **Confidence**: reasoned
- **Where**: `grep -rn "setInterval(" src/ui` outside `ui/dev` and tests: `src/ui/components/board/MatUpkeepBadge.jsx:90` (2 s, plus `:89` a recompute on every `INVENTORY_UPDATED`, `TILE_CHANGED`, `TOKEN_DEPLETED`, each running `computeUpkeepSummary()` over every Token, `src/systems/board/UpkeepSummary.js:102`, which includes `spawnerStatus` and R2's floor scan, with no change-signature gate unlike `MatCapBadge`), `MatCapBadge.jsx:236`, `WorkPlateLayer.jsx:206`, `HeroBubbleLayer.jsx:201` (500 ms), `FlagLayer.jsx:262` (500 ms, tooltip shown), `IdlePlate.jsx:50` (250 ms, hovered), `TokenCard.jsx:462` (1 s, card open; and `:511-518`: `ArtWindow`'s `useLayoutEffect(fit)` with no dependency list reads `offsetWidth`/`offsetHeight` on every render, so every clock tick), `PassiveProductionTooltip.jsx:46`, `src/ui/components/combat/FightPanelPopup.jsx:143-147` (1 s `reconcile`/`adopt` for every fight window, minimised ones included), `src/ui/components/dock/DockHeroFigure.jsx:380` (1 s, hover), `src/ui/components/drawer/UpkeepSummaryPanel.jsx:32` (2 s plus the same three events, `setSummary(new object)` each time), `src/ui/components/notifications/ItemStubColumn.jsx:135,153` (1 s per pinned stub and for the open stub), `src/ui/components/base/ArrowScrollList.jsx:159` (hold-to-scroll only), `src/ui/modals/SlotSelectionModal.jsx:58` (30 s); none reads `isDrawingPaused()` or `document.hidden` (grep above)
- **What**: Each poller runs on a timer whether or not anything it shows has moved; most set a primitive (so React bails out when it is unchanged, and the cost is the recompute, not a render), some set a fresh object (`UpkeepSummaryPanel`, a render each time), one sets nothing (`FightPanelPopup`, CR4-054), and several run only while their panel is open. ARCHITECTURE §5.4 says sixteen "outside `ui/dev`": its count includes `components/dev/AnimationStudioModal.jsx:44`, a dev tool.
- **Evidence**: As cited; `baseline/soak.txt` hidden rows: `task ms/s` 8 with `fps` 0; `MatCapBadge.jsx` keys its refresh on `capSignature` while `MatUpkeepBadge` and `UpkeepSummaryPanel` do not.

- **Verified by**: Sonnet verifier, 2026-11-07: partly; corrected. Recounted: 16 `setInterval` under `src/ui` outside `ui/dev`, one of them the dev-only Animation Studio, so fifteen in the game; every interval length matches. The upkeep badge's per-event recompute (`UPKEEP_EVENTS` is a superset of the three named) and the summary panel's fresh object confirmed. "A render and usually a commit" was overstated: most pollers set a primitive and bail out; rewritten.

### CR4-052  The mat's Token and hero projections are rebuilt on every walking step
- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / M / low
- **Confidence**: reasoned
- **Where**: `src/ui/components/board/MatBoard.jsx:163-188` (`tokensRaw`: on `ENEMIES_WALKED` (every tick an enemy steps) it maps every Token into a fresh object and, for each walker, builds an `others` array of every other Token's and flag's `y` for `walkerSortY`: O(Tokens × walkers)), `:210-262` (`heroesRaw`: on `HEROES_WALKED` (every tick a hero walks), `HERO_MOVED`, `TILE_CHANGED`, `ALERT_CHANGED` and `HEROES_UPDATED` (every XP grant, R3's CR4-044) it rebuilds every hero's record: `Flags.statusOf`, `HeroMotion.bodyView`, `roster.find` (O(heroes) each), `strikesLive`, `hitsOnAttack(hitSkillOf(getTokenType(...)))`), `src/ui/components/board/FlagLayer.jsx` (`projectFlags` on `HEROES_WALKED`, a `Flags.statusOf` and a roster lookup per flag); `src/ui/hooks/useGameState.js:107-121` and `structuralShare.js` (the result is compared and shared, so unchanged parts keep identity and the render is skipped)
- **What**: The design is right: walkers follow the engine without a render, and `shareEqual` stops a re-render when nothing visible changed. But the selectors themselves run on every listed event, and while anything walks that is every tick. Each run allocates the whole projection and, for the hero one, asks the flag system for every hero's status (which looks up camps, claims and bodies). On the realistic board with three heroes walking that is a few hundred allocations and a dozen status derivations per tick, discarded by the comparison. The tick-commits bench measured 0.57 commits per tick on S2 after round 3; the selector cost happens before any commit and is not in that number.
- **Evidence**: As cited; `MatBoard.jsx:156-162`'s comment describes the walker design the selectors serve.

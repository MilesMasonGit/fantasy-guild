## Session summary (for the owner)

**What this area is.** The playmat itself: where a Token may stand and how it is nudged or pushed,
which Token each hero's flag claims and how they walk to it, every Token's work cycle (inputs,
charges, output, XP), saplings growing and veins refilling, spawners, fights on the mat, loot on the
floor, and the buffs neighbours give each other. It is the largest area of the game and runs ten
times a second, and again 86,400 times for a day's catch-up.

**How healthy it is.** Sound, and in places excellent. The rules read clearly, the decide/act split
in placement holds, the Token list, the neighbour list and the buff caches are versioned carefully,
the catch-up plays through the same code, and the readers found no double-fire, no drifting clock
and no random draw that depends on the step size. The engine bench puts a whole tick at 0.31 ms on
the realistic board and 0.77 ms on the torture board. What the review found is cost and tidiness,
not broken play: expensive room searches that run on the tick for a Token with nowhere to put its
product, per-Token work recomputed every tick that a per-type cache would skip, two caches that
keep entries for Tokens long gone, and one real bug in moving a Token onto a copy of itself.

**The three findings that matter most.**
1. **CR4-018**: the search for "is there room on the mat for this Token?" runs on the tick for every
   station whose product has nowhere to land and for every blocked Foundation, and the memo that
   should stop it being repeated is wiped by every step a walking enemy takes. The cap boards'
   worst ticks (7 ms on 240 Tokens, 16 ms on 256, against a 0.5 ms median) are the place to look
   for it, beside the spawner re-search already filed as T-326.
2. **CR4-027**: drag a Token onto another of the same kind to top up its charges, and when every
   charge fits, the dragged Token stays where it was, empty, and is told it moved. A player sees a
   zero-charge ghost with an "Out of charges" mark.
3. **CR4-019** and **CR4-020**: for every Token a hero is working, every tick, the engine rebuilds
   that station's recipe list from the whole recipe table, rebuilds its neighbours' tag maps whether or
   not the recipe needs them, and counts the loot on the floor once per input. Small on a live tick (there are at most a
   dozen worked Tokens); it is the catch-up, 86,400 ticks for a day, where these add up, and the
   30-second target for a day away is the budget they eat.

**Counts.** 15 findings: 7 `perf` (all reasoned, P2 or P3), 1 `memory`, 2 `bug`, 4 `cleanup`,
1 `docs`. 0 `owner-decision`. All 15 verified by two second readers (Sonnet, read-only), who corrected
six details (counts, a line range, two overstated mechanisms), folded in; none refuted.

**For the director to measure first.** A CPU profile of the catch-up on the realistic board
(`node --expose-gc --cpu-prof bench/catchup/time.mjs --hours=6`, `bench/README.md` "Catch-up
timing") would rank CR4-019, 020, 021 and 022 by what they actually cost per step before anyone
touches them; a second profile of C240 at its worst tick (`bench:profile` on the cap boards, or
the engine bench with `--scenario=C240`) would show whether CR4-018 is the p99. Nothing here should
be fixed on reasoning alone, and every fix here must pass the bench's work check.

**What the readers found healthy (so nobody re-checks it).** The Token list is rebuilt only when a
Token arrives or leaves (`BoardState.tokens`, membership-versioned); the neighbour list replays the
move journal rather than clearing on every step (`nearby.neighbourIds`); the buff caches
(`TileModifiers`: grow times, gates, camp reaches) key on aggregator and registry versions; the
spawner census and the timed-change plans key on the registry version; `FightRoom` state is a
`WeakMap` per board and pruned every tick; every clamp reads the live mat size; placement draws no
random numbers; one fight per hero and per enemy is enforced; the cascade guard on triggers is
structural; passive production is closed-form with a carried remainder.

## Findings

### CR4-018  The room searches run on the tick, and the memo that should stop them is wiped by every enemy step
- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P2 / M / medium
- **Confidence**: reasoned (the mechanism is read from the code; the cap boards' p99 is the number to confirm it against)
- **Where**: `src/systems/board/BoardRunner.js:166,679` (`hasRoomForTokenOutputs`, asked at completion and then every tick while the Token holds `NO_ROOM`) → `src/systems/board/Placement.js:327-335` (`hasRoomForProduct`) → `src/systems/board/MatPlacement.js:436-475` (`findSpotAnywhere`: after the memoised near search, a grid of about 30,000 candidate points is built, sorted, and tested until one is legal, with no memo); `BoardRunner.js:673-676` → `src/systems/board/Foundations.js:43-49` (`hasRoomToBuild` runs `forceSpot`, the push solver, every tick for a blocked Foundation) → `MatPlacement.js:681-695` (`relax` rebuilds a body list from every Token per call); `MatPlacement.js:338-345` (`roomVersion` stamps `BoardState.moveCount()`, so the failure memo at `:331,:348` empties on any move) with `src/systems/board/EnemyMotion.js:183` (`setTokenPoint` per step) and `src/systems/board/BoardState.js:367-377` (every move is journalled, so the count rises)
- **What**: Two tick-path callers ask the most expensive question the mat has. A station whose recipe makes a Token (a Sapling, a boat) asks "is there room anywhere?" when its cycle completes, and, finding none, holds at `NO_ROOM` and asks again every tick (`BoardRunner.js:679`). The near search is memoised on failure, but the whole-mat half (`findSpotAnywhere`) is not: it allocates and sorts tens of thousands of candidate points on every miss, and when any Token on the mat carries a `Cannot` rule every candidate that passes the crowding test builds a projected board (`legalIn`, `MatPlacement.js:309-311`). A Foundation whose build has no room re-runs the push solver (up to 30 passes over a fresh copy of every Token) every tick. The memo that should make repeats free keys on `moveCount`, and an enemy stepping aside for a fight bumps it every tick it walks, so on any mat with a walking enemy the memo holds nothing; the same wipe is why the spawners in T-326 re-search. The player sees nothing but a slow tick: on the 240- and 256-Token boards the bench's median tick is about 0.55 ms and its worst 7.4 ms and 15.9 ms.
- **Evidence**: `bench/baseline.json`: `C240 p50 0.566, p99 7.379`; `C256 p50 0.555, p99 15.874`; `S2 p50 0.310, p99 0.746`. Code as cited; `MatPlacement.js:325-326`'s own comment: "A move anywhere forgets". `findSpotAnywhere` has no `memo`/`failedSearches` read after the `findSpot` call (`:438-475`). T-326 (6 Nov) measured 16 spawners' misses at about 0.65 ms each in one tick on C240.
- **Fix sketch**: (1) Memoise `findSpotAnywhere`'s failure under the same key as `findSpot` (it reads the same three things), so a `NO_ROOM` station asks once per board change, not once per tick. (2) Make the memo survive a move that cannot matter: replay the move journal as `nearby.neighbourIds` does (`BoardState.eachMoveSince`) and forget only keys whose point lies within the search's reach of either end of the move, or at minimum stamp with the membership version plus a count of moves within the reach rather than all moves. (3) For a blocked Foundation, remember the refusal under the same memo (a push solver's answer depends on the same three things plus the fixed set). Must not change: where any Token lands (the bench's `S4` push storm fingerprint and `tokensHash` on every scenario), the `Cannot` rule's exactness (a memo must never return a stale success: only failures are memoised, as today), T-326's own fix. **Hypothesis to test**: C240 and C256 p99 drop toward their p50 with no change to any fingerprint; T-326's 0.65 ms misses stop recurring on an unchanged board.
- **Verified by**: verifier (Sonnet, read-only, 2026-11-07): confirmed. Read BoardRunner.js:672-681 inside the per-Token pass, hasRoomForProduct, findSpotAnywhere (memo.add and failureMemo appear only in findSpot, :379-392), forceSpot, relax (PUSH_PASSES 30), roomVersion/failureMemo, setTokenPoint and journalMove, EnemyMotion.js:183; bench/baseline.json C240 0.566/7.379, C256 0.555/15.874, S2 0.310/0.746 match; T-326 matches. Only the link from p99 to this mechanism is untested, as the finding says.

### CR4-019  A station's recipe list is rebuilt from the whole recipe table on every call, including every tick it is worked
- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / S / low
- **Confidence**: reasoned
- **Where**: `src/config/registries/recipePoolRegistry.js:109-121` (`getSkillRecipePool` → `skillRecipes`: `RECIPES.filter(...)` then a second `filter`), `:160-173` (`recipesForToken` → `recipesForStation`: a third `filter`; `stationSkillOf` on the way), no cache in the file (grep `Map(|cache|memo|registryVersion`: none); callers per worked Token per tick: `src/systems/board/RecipeResolver.js:136` (`resolveRecipe` → `recipesForToken`) via `src/systems/board/WorkCheck.js:117` (`fixableReason` → `effectiveIO`) via `BoardRunner.js:597`; `src/systems/board/StationRecipe.js:48-52` (`selectedRecipe` → `poolFor(...).find`) reached from `validateSelection` inside the same resolve, so one resolve of a station with a selected recipe builds the pool twice; `StationRecipe.js:112-121` (`workConfigOf` → `selectedRecipe` → the pool, for every Foundation every tick, `BoardRunner.js:564`); `src/systems/board/Flags.js:387` (every candidate a flag considers)
- **What**: The recipe table is content: it changes only when the registry version moves (a CMS sync, a test fixture). Yet every question "what recipes does this station have?" filters the whole table two or three times and allocates the results, and the engine asks it for every worked Token on every tick (through the per-tick "can this cycle still run?" check), for every Foundation on every tick, and for every Token a flag looks at. With a dozen worked Tokens and a few hundred recipes that is a few thousand predicate calls and a few dozen arrays per tick; a day's catch-up multiplies it by 86,400. Nothing is wrong; it is the one registry lookup on the hot path that has no memo while every neighbour of it (`TimedChanges.planOf`, `Camps.enemiesOf`, `SpawnerSystem.familyOf`) has one.
- **Evidence**: As cited; `RecipeResolver.js:30-31` says the neighbour list "is cached per instance … because this module asks it several times per station per tick", which is true of neighbours and shows the pattern the pool lacks.
- **Fix sketch**: In `recipePoolRegistry.js`, memoise `recipesForStation(skill, kind)` and `recipesForFoundation` per key on `registryVersion()` plus `RECIPES.length` (recipes are only ever pushed, `:238`), returning the same frozen array each time; `selectedRecipe` then finds in a shared array. Must not change: the pool's order (it is the order the station card lists recipes in and the order the resolver falls through), the bench fingerprint. **Hypothesis to test**: the catch-up CPU profile shows `skillRecipes`/`recipesForStation` in its top functions before and gone after.
- **Verified by**: verifier (Sonnet, read-only, 2026-11-07): confirmed. recipePoolRegistry.js has no Map, cache, memo or version; three filters as described plus recipesForFoundation; callers on the tick path as cited; two small qualifications folded in (the double build needs a selected recipe; a Foundation builds the pool only with a selection).

### CR4-020  For every worked Token, every tick: the neighbours' tags rebuilt whether or not the recipe needs them, and the loot floor counted once per input
- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / M / low
- **Confidence**: reasoned
- **Where**: neighbours: `src/systems/board/RecipeResolver.js:76-91` (`checkAcceptedTokens`: `contextTiersAround` and `typesNear`), `:102-107` (`unmetContext`: `contextTiersAround` again), `src/systems/board/Charges.js:233-244` (`planContextCharges`: `contextProvidersAround` built before the loop over requirements, so it runs even when the recipe needs no context), each over `nearby.neighbourIds` with `src/systems/effects/statements.js:555-575` (`getProvidedTagsWithTiers`: a fresh object per neighbour per call); the floor: `src/systems/board/InputAllocator.js:27-29` (`availableOf` → `SpriteLayer.countOnBoard`), `src/systems/board/SpriteLayer.js:502-506` (`getSprites().filter(...).reduce(...)`: a filtered array per item per call), called from `InputAllocator.checkInputs` (`WorkCheck.js:134-137`, per input per worked Token per tick), `src/systems/board/SpawnerSystem.js:330` (`missingUpkeep`, twice per spawner per tick), `src/systems/board/Growth.js` (`available`, cached per item per epoch, the epoch bumped once a tick at `TimedChanges.js:498`: one floor count per watched item per tick)
- **What**: `WorkCheck.fixableReason` runs for every worked Token on every tick to confirm its cycle may still advance (the no-partial-cycles rule), and again at completion. Inside it the charge planner walks the Token's neighbour list and rebuilds each neighbour's tag map before it looks at whether the recipe has any context requirement at all (the resolver's two walks return early when the Token has no `acceptedTokens` or the recipe no context, so a Token with both is walked three times and a typical worked Token once, every tick, for an answer that is almost always "no requirements"), and the input check counts the loot on the floor by filtering the whole sprite list once per input. Both are per-type or per-item facts that change only when a Token arrives, leaves or moves, or a sprite is added or taken. Live, with a dozen worked Tokens and forty floor stacks, this is tens of microseconds; in the catch-up it is tens of microseconds 86,400 times, and the sprite filter grows with the floor, which with a full Bank is where loot waits by design.
- **Evidence**: As cited; `Charges.js:234` runs `contextProvidersAround(instanceId)` before `for (const req of contextRequirementsOf(...))`, so a recipe with no `requiresContext` still scans. `SpriteLayer.js:20-25, 434-442` describe the over-cap floor as the steady state with a full Bank.
- **Fix sketch**: (1) One tag map per station per tick, or per `neighbourIds` cache entry: compute `{ tiers, types, providers }` once in `RecipeResolver` and hand it to `Charges.planCycle`, or cache it in `nearby.js` beside the id list (same invalidation). (2) Return early from `planContextCharges` when there are no requirements. (3) Keep a `Map<itemId, quantity>` of floor stacks in `SpriteLayer`, updated where sprites are added, merged and taken (`addSprite`, `takeSprite`, `absorbSprite`), and have `countOnBoard` read it. Must not change: any answer (the bench fingerprint holds Bank items and Tokens), the order of inputs checked. **Hypothesis to test**: the catch-up profile's share for `getProvidedTagsWithTiers`, `contextTiersAround` and `countOnBoard` before and after.
- **Verified by**: verifier (Sonnet, read-only, 2026-11-07): confirmed after a correction (folded in): only planContextCharges always walks the neighbours; checkAcceptedTokens and unmetContext return early without acceptedTokens or context, so the walk is one per typical worked Token and three for one with both. countOnBoard is filter().reduce() with no per-item map; checkInputs once per input per tick; missingUpkeep up to twice per spawner; Growth.available is cached per epoch, one count per item per tick.

- **Confidence**: reasoned
- **Where**: `src/systems/board/BlockUpkeep.js:22-24,52-54` (`costedStatements(def)` is `statementsOf(def).filter(...)`, called from `tickUpkeep` for every Token on the mat every tick at `BoardRunner.js:522`, before every other guard; almost no Token has upkeep); `src/systems/board/TimedChanges.js:388-392` (`activeHandlers`: `plan.fixed.slice()` for every Token whose type has a `respawn` block, resting or not, and a re-filter of the whole handler table for every turned Token) and `:410` (`advance`: `new Set()` per timed Token per step); `src/systems/board/TriggerSystem.js:139-141` (`triggeredStatements`: a filtered array per neighbour per event, and per Token on the mat on every Bank change in `handleGlobalItemThreshold`, `:517-527`); `src/systems/board/HeroMotion.js:122-124,209-216` (`guildHallPoint` and `homeSpot` scan every Token for the Hall and map the roster, called from `destinationOf` `:316,320` for every hero at home every tick); `src/systems/board/Flags.js:555-558` (`plantingOrder`: `Object.keys(flags).sort(...)` every tick)

Must not change: handler order in `TimedChanges` (it is the table's order), the bench fingerprint, `TickRedraws.test.js`. **Hypothesis to test**: the catch-up profile's allocation share; `TimedChanges.advance` and `tickUpkeep` self time before and after.

### CR4-022  A tick in which several spawners fire rebuilds the spawner census and re-sorts the Token list after each spawn
- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / S / low
- **Confidence**: reasoned
- **Where**: `src/systems/board/SpawnerSystem.js:243-262` (`ensureCensus`: a full pass over every Token whenever the membership version, the tether version or the registry version moved), `:400-402` (a spawn calls `BoardState.setTether` after the Token has landed, which bumps the tether version on top of the membership bump the landing made), `src/systems/board/BoardState.js:236-239` (`setTether` bumps), `:425-436` (`tokens()` rebuilds and sorts its list on a membership change)
- **What**: The census is the right idea: one scan shared by every spawner's cap check and the alert sweep. But each spawn invalidates it (the landing bumps the membership version; an enemy camp's spawn also bumps the tether version, which adds no further rebuild since nothing reads the census in between), and the next spawner in the same tick rebuilds it, and the Token list it reads from is also rebuilt and sorted. On the cap boards the spawners' laps line up (T-326: 16 Forests and Quarries miss in the same tick every 5 s), so the tick in which they all fire does about K census passes and K sorts of N Tokens. Live, a burst of spawns is rare after the opening; in a catch-up, every 5 s step on a cap board is such a tick.
- **Evidence**: As cited; `SpawnerSystem.js:238-241` comment: "a spawn earlier in the same pass bumps membership and the next spawner re-counts" (the design accepts one re-count per spawn; the list sort is on top). The verifier checked that the tether bump at `:400-402` fires only for enemy camps and causes no extra rebuild.
- **Fix sketch**: Let `ensureCensus` patch itself for a single add (one entry in `countByType`, one in `tethered`, one push to `spawners`) instead of rescanning. `BoardState.tokens()`'s sort could insert the new Token at the end (arrival order is `placedAt` ascending and a new Token has the highest) instead of re-sorting. Must not change: arrival order (the frozen list is the order every pass iterates in; the bench fingerprint depends on it), the one-scan-per-change guarantee `TetherCensus.test.js` checks. **Hypothesis to test**: C240's worst tick in the engine bench before and after, with fingerprints identical.
- **Verified by**: verifier (Sonnet, read-only, 2026-11-07): confirmed after a correction (folded in): the tether bump causes no second rebuild (nothing reads the census between the landing and setTether) and fires only for enemy camps, so it is one rescan and one list sort per spawn; those two are as cited and on the tick path through TimedChanges.

- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / S / low
- **Confidence**: reasoned
- **Where**: `src/config/registries/triggerRegistry.js:85-93` (`CYCLE_COMPLETE`, nearby-scoped, on `BOARD_EVENTS.CYCLE_COMPLETE`) and `:184-190` (`ITEM_PRODUCED`, nearby-scoped, on the same bus event); `src/systems/board/TriggerSystem.js:544-565` (`init`: one bus subscription per trigger definition, each calling `handleNearby`), `:463-485` (`handleNearby`: `tokensWithin` over every Token, then `triggeredStatements` per neighbour); `:517-527` (`handleGlobalItemThreshold`: every Token on the mat, `getTokenType` and a filtered array each, on every `INVENTORY_UPDATED`); `:351` (its subscription)

Must not change: firing order across kinds (today's order is definition order within one event's listeners; keep it), the cascade guard, the bench fingerprint (`randomDraws`: a trigger that fires draws; the scan itself draws nothing). **Hypothesis to test**: the catch-up profile's `handleNearby` self time.
- **Verified by**: verifier (Sonnet, read-only, 2026-11-07): confirmed. Lines 88 and 186 nearby-scoped on one event, 208 self-scoped; one subscription per definition; tokensWithin is a linear scan with no spatial index; the global kind has no early exit; CYCLE_COMPLETE published from BoardRunner.js:380 and BoardCombat.js:468; S8 cycles 2100 per hour matches.

### CR4-025  A Token that leaves the mat keeps its buff cache until the next load
- **Tag**: memory
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / S / low
- **Confidence**: reasoned (the soak's heap diff bounds it: small)
- **Where**: `src/systems/board/TileModifiers.js:452-458` (`rebuildToken`: the only place that deletes an entry from `aggregators`, `growTimes`, `gates` and `campReaches`, and only when called with the id of a Token no longer on the mat); every caller passes ids of Tokens that are on the mat: `:743,761,767` (`rebuildTokens`: `ids` from `rebuildAround` → `nearby.tokensAround`, which lists Tokens on the mat, `nearby.js:124-134`; `changed.ids` from `layoutChanges`, which iterates `BoardState.tokens()`), `:754,799` (`BoardState.tokens()`); `:95-103` (`clearAll`, run by `rebuildAll` on a load, a Region swap or a Near-radius change)
- **What**: Every Token on the mat gets a `ModifierAggregator` (two `Map`s, a `Set`, its inbound modifiers) the first time its neighbourhood is rebuilt, and a `growTimes` and `gates` entry if it ever grew, rested or was gated. When the Token leaves (a vein used up, a goblin killed, a sapling that became a tree, a sprite-replaced bearer), nothing deletes those entries: the only deleting path is reached with the ids of Tokens that are still there. So the four maps grow by one entry per Token that ever left the board since the last load.

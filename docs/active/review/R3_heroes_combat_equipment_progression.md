## Session summary (for the owner)

**What this area is.** Everything about a hero as a person: their record, skills and XP, what
they carry and what it lends them, how they eat and drink, how a fight is fought swing by swing,
what breaks and what drops, the statuses and potions that ride on them, and the Guild Hall's
stars, the discoveries and the Encyclopedia's "where does this come from" engine.

**How healthy it is.** Good, with one hot spot and a layer of old residue. The combat engine is
small, deterministic (its random draws are documented one by one) and well guarded against a
fight being joined twice or a defeat resolved twice. The effect grammar and the three-bucket
maths are carefully explained. What the review found: the hero's whole loadout is re-expanded
from item definitions on every per-tick work-time read (round 3 filed this as "latent"; it is
not, it runs ten times a second for every worked Token and 86,400 times for a day away); the
bucket maths is written by hand in three places that have already drifted; two save fields grow
on every item gain and are read by nothing; and a ring of card-era code (thorns, combat triggers,
card assignments, a dead aggregator cache and expiry check) that nothing reaches.

**The three findings that matter most.**
1. **CR4-033**: every tick, for every worked Token, the game rebuilds the working hero's entire
   loadout (every item's rules, scaled and stamped, plus their perks and potions) to read one
   number, the work time. Nothing is wrong with the answer; it is the single most expensive
   repeated thing the hero code does, and the catch-up multiplies it by 86,400.
2. **CR4-036**: the rule that turns "+10 %", "×2" and "+3" into a number is written four times,
   and the copies disagree on two edge cases. No shipped content hits the disagreements today,
   so nothing is wrong on screen; the next authored effect that does will be wrong in one place
   and right in another with no warning.
3. **CR4-040**: every item that reaches the Bank writes two records into the save that nothing
   ever reads (where it came from, and a "New!" flag that is never cleared), and announces it on
   an event with no listener. The save grows for nothing; the fix is a deletion.

**Counts.** 13 findings: 3 `perf` (reasoned, P2 to P3), 3 `bug`, 6 `cleanup`, 1 `docs`.
0 `owner-decision`; one design question for the owner is parked in STATUS (whether XP bonuses
should apply to kills, CR4-037). All 13 verified by two second readers (Sonnet, read-only), who
corrected eight details (counts, line numbers, one export wrongly called dead, one citation), folded
in; none refuted.

**For the director to measure first.** The same catch-up CPU profile R2 asked for
(`node --expose-gc --cpu-prof bench/catchup/time.mjs --hours=6`) ranks CR4-033 and CR4-034
against R2's findings; `loadoutStatements` and `statementsFromEntry` should be near the top if
CR4-033 is what it looks like. Every fix in this area must pass the bench's work check; CR4-035
and CR4-037 change the random stream or the XP totals on purpose and must say so.

**What the readers found healthy.** Live-effect bearers are rebuilt every 3 s from the roster
and the live fights, so a Token that left the mat drops out; `FightRoom` and the fight map are
cleared on a load and a Region swap; purchases of a Hall star are bounds-checked and the price
read at the right rank; `recompute` is idempotent; the collection log's writers are plain
property writes; the swing's random draws (miss, crit, fraction, breakage) are each documented
and in a fixed order; `rollFraction` draws only when the blow has a fraction.

## Findings

### CR4-033  The working hero's whole loadout is rebuilt on every per-tick work-time read
- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P2 / M / medium
- **Confidence**: reasoned (the mechanism is read from the code; the catch-up profile is the number to confirm it against)
- **Where**: `src/systems/board/BoardRunner.js:719` (`TileModifiers.workTimeOf` for every worked Token every tick) → `src/systems/board/TileModifiers.js:816-840` (`resolveAxis`: six aggregator scans, nine arrays) → `:1022-1067` (`heroContributions`: `[...HeroEffects.loadoutStatements(hero), ...LiveEffects.modifierStatements(hero, instanceId)]` rebuilt per call, kept only for `PROVIDES` of one type); `src/systems/hero/HeroEffects.js:45-60` (`lendingEntries`: `getGrid`, `getHeroWeaponSlot`, per slot `slotBacked`, `getItem`, `meetsGearRequirements`), `:70-95` (`loadoutRefs`: a `Map` and `effectRefsOf` per item), `:111-132` (`loadoutStatements`: `statementsFromEntry` per effect, a second expansion when only some items are worn, a spread per statement), `:142-154` (`perkStatements` → `HeroPerks.earnedPerkRefs`, `HeroPerks.js:44-53`: a `Map` and array per read); `src/systems/effects/effectLibrary.js:128-147` (`statementsFromEntry`: a new object per statement with `effectTitle` building a string); `src/systems/effects/LiveEffects.js:470-479` (`liveStatements`: `statementsFromEntry` again, then a filter)
- **What**: Round 3 found this (CR3-253) and the planning agent filed it as latent (T-078). It is not latent. `WORK_TIME` is the one per-tick axis `TileModifiers` does not version-cache (`growTimeOf`, `campReachOf` and the nearby gate are), so every tick, for every Token with a hero on it, the hero's loadout is expanded from the item library: every item's effect references merged, every effect's statements copied and scaled and titled, the perks merged, the live potions expanded, all to keep the handful of statements that say `Provides WORK_TIME`. The stated reason for reading live (`TileModifiers.js:1024-1030`) is that the Bank's stock of a consumable decides whether its slot counts (`slotBacked`). That is true and the fix must keep it; it does not require re-expanding the item library. Live, with a dozen worked Tokens, this is a few hundred allocations a tick; in a day's catch-up it runs 86,400 times per worked Token, which is where the 30-second budget goes.
- **Evidence**: As cited; `TileModifiers.js:922-936` (`growTimeOf`) shows the versioned cache pattern the other axes use and `WORK_TIME` lacks; `TICKETS.md:152` lists T-078 as "latent".
- **Fix sketch**: Memoise `loadoutStatements(hero)` per hero on a key of what it reads: the grid (`hero.equipment` identity or `hero._rev`), the perk-bearing levels (`hero.skills` levels, or `_rev` bumped on level-up), and a Bank version counter for `slotBacked` (`InventoryStore` bumps one on any quantity change; `slotBacked` reads only "at least one", so the key can be the set of consumable item ids with stock, or simply the counter); keep `liveStatements` live (the effects list is small and changes on its own clock) or version it on `hero.effects` mutation. Then `heroContributions` is a filter over a cached array. Must not change: the answer (the bench fingerprint, `heroXp`, `tokensHash`), the Bank-stock rule, perks reached mid-catch-up (the key must include levels). **Hypothesis to test**: the catch-up profile's self time in `loadoutStatements`, `statementsFromEntry`, `effectTitle` and `lendingEntries` before and after; T-078 closes with this.
- **Verified by**: verifier (Sonnet, read-only, 2026-11-07): confirmed. BoardRunner.js:719 → workTimeOf (:855) → resolveAxis with six scans and the arrays; heroContributions rebuilds the two statement lists per call and returns early only with no worker; traced the per-call allocations in lendingEntries, loadoutRefs, loadoutStatements (second expansion when partly worn, a spread per statement), perkStatements and liveStatements; version caches exist for growTimeOf (:906), campReachOf (:953) and nearbyGateMet (:992) and not for WORK_TIME; TICKETS.md:152 lists T-078 as latent.
- **State**: ticket T-078 (triaged 2026-11-07)

### CR4-034  The swung weapon is derived up to seven times per swing (more with fight moments) and once more per tick
- **Tag**: perf
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / S / low
- **Confidence**: reasoned
- **Where**: `src/utils/CombatFormulas.js:40-56` (`getHeroWeapon` → `getHeroWeaponSlot`: `getEquippedEntries(hero)`, which maps and filters the grid into fresh objects (`src/config/registries/equipmentConstants.js:73-77`), then `getItem` and `meetsGearRequirements` per weapon); its callers per hero swing in `src/systems/combat/CombatAttackProcessor.js:75-140`: `heroMissChance` (`CombatFormulas.js:194-196` → `getHeroCombatLevel` → `getHeroCombatStyle` → `getHeroWeapon`), `heroBlow` (`:331-336`: `getHeroCombatStyle` once, `heroHitParts` → `heroDamageType` → `getHeroWeapon`, `heroBaseDamage` → `getHeroWeapon` and `getHeroCombatLevel` → `getHeroWeapon`), `nextArrowOf` twice (`:94` and inside `spendArrow`, `src/systems/combat/Arrows.js:40-46,53-57` → `shootsArrows` → `getHeroCombatStyle`); per tick per fight `heroAttackSpeedMs` (`CombatFormulas.js:122-124`, from `CombatProcessor.js:77`); per enemy swing `enemyBlow` and `enemyMissChance` each derive the hero's style again (`:344-350`, `:198-201`)
- **What**: Every combat formula is written to be pure and to read the hero's hands live, which is right: a weapon broken mid-fight must stop lending at once. But each formula re-derives "which weapon is swung" from scratch, and a swing calls six or more of them, so the grid is mapped into entry objects, the weapon definition looked up and its requirement checked seven times for one landed ranged blow (three on a miss; the verifier's count), once more for every fight moment the swing runs (`FightMoments.js:66` → `loadoutStatements` → `getHeroWeaponSlot`), and once more every tick to know the swing's pace. Round 3 measured the combat engine at about 2 KB allocated per fight per tick and 3.8 KB per swing (CR3-259; T-026 keeps "small per-tick allocations in combat" as a ride-along). This is the structural reason for most of it. Fights are bounded by heroes, so live it is small; in a catch-up every swing of every fight replays it.
- **Evidence**: As cited; `getEquippedEntries` allocates per call; no memo in `CombatFormulas.js` (grep `Map(|cache|memo`: none).
- **Fix sketch**: Derive once per swing: a `fighterView(hero)` (`{ weapon, slot, style, level }`) computed at the top of `handleHeroAttack` and `enemySwing` and passed into `heroBlow`, `heroMissChance`, `heroCritChance`, `nextArrowOf`; keep the existing functions as thin wrappers that build the view for other callers. Must not change: any number or draw (the bench fingerprint); the live read within a swing (the view is per swing, never cached across ticks). Joins T-026. **Hypothesis to test**: the catch-up profile on a fight-heavy board (`enemies80` or the S8 hour: 240 kills) for `getEquippedEntries` and `getHeroWeaponSlot`.
- **Verified by**: verifier (Sonnet, read-only, 2026-11-07): confirmed with the count corrected (folded in): seven weapon derivations on a landed ranged blow, three on a miss, one per fight moment through loadoutStatements, one per tick for the swing pace; getEquippedEntries allocates per call; no memo in CombatFormulas.js; the cited line ranges are right.
- **State**: ticket T-347 (triaged 2026-11-07)

### CR4-035  Every enemy swing draws a random number to choose between one hero
- **Tag**: cleanup
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / S / low
- **Confidence**: verified
- **Where**: `src/systems/combat/CombatProcessor.js:36` (`const assignedHeroIds = [heroId];`: every fight is one-on-one), `src/systems/combat/CombatAttackProcessor.js:197-198` (`processEnemyAttack`: `assignedHeroIds[Math.floor(Math.random() * assignedHeroIds.length)]`)
- **What**: The enemy's target is picked at random from the heroes in the fight, a leftover from the card era when several heroes could share one. Today the list always holds exactly one hero (`BoardCombat` enforces one fight per hero and per enemy), so the draw always yields index 0 and decides nothing, but it consumes one `Math.random` per enemy swing. Like R2's CR4-032 (a status rolled twice), it moves the shared random stream, so every later roll in the tick is a different number than it would be and the bench's `randomDraws` counts it. No outcome a player sees depends on it.
- **Evidence**: As cited; `grep -rn "assignedHeroIds" src/systems/combat` shows the list is built once from a single id and never appended.
- **Fix sketch**: `const targetHeroId = assignedHeroIds[0];` (or pass `heroId` straight through). **Changes the random stream**: expect `WORK CHANGED` on every fighting scenario (S2, S3, S8, C240, C256) and accept it knowingly; the ticket must say so and the baseline be re-saved after. Nothing else changes.
- **Verified by**: verifier (Sonnet, read-only, 2026-11-07): confirmed. assignedHeroIds is built once as a one-element array and never pushed to or reassigned; the one draw at CombatAttackProcessor.js:198 always yields index 0 and consumes one Math.random per enemy swing.
- **State**: ticket T-368 (triaged 2026-11-07)

### CR4-036  The bucket maths is written four times, and the copies disagree
- **Tag**: bug
- **Priority / Effort / Risk**: P3 / S / medium
- **Confidence**: verified (the divergences are read from the code; no shipped content hits them today)
- **Where**: the aggregator's classification: `src/systems/effects/ModifierAggregator.js:175-184` (`query`: anything not `multiplier`/`percentage` is flat, a bare entry included), `:203-211` (`collectMultipliers`), `:222-231` (`collectPercentages`: anything not `multiplier`/`flat`/`additive` is a percentage, a bare entry included; a 0 is dropped), `:272-290` (`_forEachMatching`: category matches `ALL`, the exact category or a parent through `_isParentOf`, lower-cased); the hero's hand-written copy: `src/systems/board/TileModifiers.js:1056-1063` (`heroContributions`: exact `payload.category !== category` or skip; `flat` / `multiplier` / else percentage, a 0 kept); the ledger's copy: `src/systems/progression/ledger/rates.js:477-483` (`heroBuckets`: the same three bucket lines at `:481-483`, with a weight and a `used` set around them); a fourth category matcher: `src/systems/core/gearDefenceRule.js:107-113`; a retyped formula: `TileModifiers.js:938-941` (`growTimeOf` writes `(base + flat) * multiplier * percentage` instead of calling `applyThreeBucket`); a third formula: `src/systems/hero/SkillSystem.js:66-67` (`getXpMultiplier`: multiplier bucket × percentage bucket, no flat)
- **What**: The rule that turns authored modifiers into one number ("Final = (Base + Σflat) × Σmultipliers × (1 + Σpercentages)", `TileModifiers.js:27-31`) has one implementation in the aggregator and three more written by hand where the hero's loadout is read live. They differ: (a) a modifier whose bucket is `additive` is flat in the aggregator and a percentage in the hero path; (b) a hero rule whose category is a group (`combat`, a damage group) matches in the aggregator through `_isParentOf` and never in the hero path, which compares the strings exactly; (c) the aggregator drops a 0-valued percentage, the hero path keeps it (harmless); (d) a modifier with no bucket at all is counted by the aggregator as flat in `query` **and** as a percentage in `collectPercentages`, so `resolveAxis` would apply it twice. The effects reader checked every `Provides` payload in `data/` (138 across effects, Tokens, items, Hall stars, perks): all carry a bucket and none is `additive`, so nothing on screen is wrong today. The next effect authored without a bucket, or aimed at a skill group from an item, will be right in one reader and wrong in another, and the Encyclopedia's rate calculator (the ledger copy) will agree with neither.
- **Evidence**: As cited, each side read.
- **Fix sketch**: One exported `classifyModifier(payload)` → `{ bucket: 'flat'|'multiplier'|'percentage', value }` and one `categoryMatches(payloadCategory, asked)` in `ModifierAggregator.js`, used by `query`/`collect*`, `heroContributions`, `rates.heroBuckets` and `gearDefenceRule`; `growTimeOf` calls `applyThreeBucket` over its cached sums (same arithmetic, one formula); the CMS's palette already refuses `additive`, so the classifier can treat a bare or `additive` bucket as flat and warn once. Must not change: any resolved number for today's content (the bench fingerprint; the ledger's `calibration` figures), the aggregator's `_isParentOf` semantics. A content-audit rule (R8) that refuses a bucketless `Provides` closes the door for good.
- **Verified by**: verifier (Sonnet, read-only, 2026-11-07): confirmed on all four divergences (a) to (d) by reading both sides of each; the ledger copy shares the three bucket lines, not six (folded in); growTimeOf's arithmetic equals applyThreeBucket's (:70-74) wrapped in floorSpeedUp; gearDefenceRule is a fourth matcher; getXpMultiplier has no flat.
- **State**: ticket T-360 (triaged 2026-11-07)

XP_BONUS's palette entry has no heroOnly (the 14 heroOnly entries are :383-564); both hero writers filter on heroOnly; every addModifier caller in src and cms/src was listed and none writes XP_BONUS to a hero aggregator; addXP applies only getXpMultiplier; handleVictory passes the flat award; the work path applies the bonus through resolveAxis; data counts match.

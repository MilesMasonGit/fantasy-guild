# Fantasy Guild — Game Design Document

**Status:** Current State / Active Baseline
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-05

## 1. Game Overview
*Fantasy Guild Idle* is a simulation and resource-management game built around a "Simulation-First" philosophy, where value is a direct derivative of effort. The player sets up autonomous operations on a "Free Playmat" by managing a crew of Heroes who harvest resources, craft items, and fight enemies.

## 2. Core Gameplay Loop
The game has moved away from rigid grid placement to a **Free Playmat**. The second-to-second loop is based on spatial management and automation:
- **Managing Flags:** The player drops a "flag" (a banner with a specific radius and skill) onto the mat. The assigned Hero autonomously walks to and works all valid Tokens/nodes within that flag's radius.
- **Foundations & Tokens:** The player purchases Foundations from a Spawner Shop and places them freely on the playmat. These Foundations either spawn or upgrade into functional Tokens (e.g., crafting stations, resource nodes).
- **Automation:** Once flags are placed and Foundations are built, Heroes autonomously perform tasks, fight, and gather as long as their requirements (and the flag radius) allow.

## 3. Heroes & Skills
The game operates on the **4-Combat / 4-Basic System** for Hero capabilities:
- **Skills:** Heroes possess defined skill levels in both combat and basic gathering/crafting disciplines.
- **Autonomy:** Heroes do not need to be micromanaged per action; they rely on their assigned flag and their inherent skills to work Foundations, fight enemies, or gather resources within their radius.

## 4. The CMS & Balancing Engine
The economy is driven by a standalone CMS and solver that decouples per-cycle velocity from lifetime capacity.
- **Effort to Value:** Every token, item, and task's Expected Value (EV) is algorithmically derived from the effort required to produce it.
- **Solver Engine:** This ensures deep mechanical balance across all 22 mechanical profiles and progression stages.

## 5. Planned Features
### The Atlas System (**Planned**)
- **Map Chemistry:** The player will loot Base Maps and Modifiers, combining them in a Cartography UI to define a generation budget and procedurally create a custom Region layout.
- **Relocation & Progression:** To progress, players must abandon old regions and travel to new ones (bringing all Heroes) to secure better resources demanded by the Guild. 
- **Static Nodes:** Many tokens will become static geographic fixtures (e.g., permanent Forests or Ore deposits) generated by the Map budget, rather than freely placed items. Player-built Foundations will interact with these organic nodes.

## 7. Glossary
- **Free Playmat:** The continuous, gridless surface where gameplay occurs.
- **Token:** An interactive object on the playmat (e.g., resource node, enemy, or crafting station).
- **Foundation:** A player-placed structure that spawns or upgrades into a Token.
- **Flag:** An area-of-effect marker placed by the player, assigning a Hero to autonomously execute a specific skill within that radius.
- **Atlas:** (Planned) The system for crafting, discovering, and traveling between unique procedural Regions.
- **Region:** (Planned) A single, screen-sized map generated via the Atlas system.
